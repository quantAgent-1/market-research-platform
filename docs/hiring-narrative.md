# Hiring narrative

Use this when writing emails, LinkedIn messages, or preparing interviews. Pair with [SHOWCASE.md](../SHOWCASE.md).

## The story in one sentence

> I build and document **cost-honest quant research systems** — methodology, sector analysis, and Python tools — with the same verification discipline I would want on a professional desk.

## Three pillars (never more than three in a pitch)

1. **Epistemics** — I know what the literature can and cannot justify (replication vs tradeability, multiple testing, who pays).  
2. **Build** — I ship research software with configs, caches, selftests, and written kill criteria.  
3. **Domain** — I can work semiconductor/AI cycle mechanics and financing topology with primary-source habits.

## Elevator (30 seconds)

“I maintain a public quant research base and tool library. On the research side, I’ve written long-form, evidence-graded work on how strategies are discovered and why most published edges don’t pay after costs. On the build side, I have runnable tools for multi-name valuation, forced-flow calendars, and institutional-flow reading on semis. I’m looking for a seat where both rigorous research process and the ability to ship matter.”

## Elevator (10 seconds)

“Public portfolio: quant validation research, semis/AI analysis, and Python research tooling — valuation, flow, calendars.”

## Behavioral answers (STAR-ready themes)

| Question type | Point to |
|---------------|----------|
| “Tell me about a time you killed your own idea” | Retail-capital edge map / Phase-1 kills; any deep-research refutation |
| “How do you avoid overfitting?” | MinBTL, DSR, pre-registered constants on Korea flow sleeve |
| “How do you use AI?” | Research accuracy stack: pullers, adversarial verify, claim ledgers |
| “Show me code quality” | muval selftest, assumptions.toml, alpaca-data package layout |
| “Sector knowledge?” | Capex financing page + state-of-play tripwires |

## Red flags to avoid saying

- Any personal P&L, account size, or “I’m up/down on MU”  
- “I beat the market” without audited track record  
- “AI does my research” without verification  
- Apologizing for the wiki being large — lead with signal density instead  

## Closing ask (email)

> Happy to walk through any single artifact on a 20-minute call — research synthesis, a design doc, or a live tool demo. Which would be most useful for your team?
