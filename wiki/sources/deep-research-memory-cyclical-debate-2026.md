# Memory: Cyclical vs "New Era" — Deep-Research Brief

**Source:** Claude Code `deep-research` run `wf_e468387b-144` (2026-06-20). **Hit the session-quota wall**
mid-verification (10/25 salvaged; reset 1:40am KST) → **resumed past the reset** (`args` re-passed) → clean.
**Method:** 5 angles → 21 sources → 100 claims → 25 adversarially verified → **21 confirmed, 4 refuted →
12 findings.** Cyclical magnitudes rest on **primary** SK Hynix filings; the bull case on sell-side
(BofA/ClearBridge) + management (Micron/SK Hynix execs); the skeptic anchor on a single op-ed.
**Purpose:** adjudicate the live debate — "memory is inherently cyclical" vs "this is a new normal of
ever-expanding AI demand." **Evidence/education, not investment advice.**

## The one-paragraph verdict
**Dampened and elongated, NOT abolished — and it bifurcates.** AI/HBM is most plausibly stretching and
softening the memory cycle rather than repealing it, with **HBM structurally stickier** and **commodity
DRAM/NAND still cyclical**. The decisive lens is **epistemic asymmetry**: the cyclical case is *hard
audited fact* (SK Hynix's record FY2023 loss, only ~3 years old), while the entire "new era" case is
*forecasts and self-interested management assertions* not yet tested through a downturn. The most
defensible synthesis (ClearBridge): GenAI "could elongate and **reduce the severity**" of the cycle —
*reduce*, not eliminate.

## Confirmed findings

### The cyclical case — HARD FACT (the bear anchor)
- **SK Hynix FY2023: record operating loss ₩7.73T (−24% margin), net loss ₩9.14T** — first annual loss
  in ~10 years. [3-0, primary]
- **Q1 2023: record quarterly operating loss ₩3.4T (~$2.54B, −67% margin)** — largest since 2012 — then
  **four straight loss quarters**; thin Q4'23 return to ₩346B OP (3% margin) but *still a net loss*. The
  full peak-to-trough arc. [3-0, primary]
- **Classic "pork-cycle" glut:** collapsing prices + weak demand; inventory "all-time high"; price drop
  "largest since Q4 2008." The recovery was **narrow and AI/HBM-led** (DDR5 +>4×, HBM3 +>5× YoY in Q4'23),
  *not* broad commodity strength. [3-0]

### The "new era" / structural-bull case — FORECASTS + management assertions
- **BofA:** 2026 "**supercycle similar to the 1990s boom**," DRAM revenue **+51%**, NAND **+45%**, HBM
  market **$54.6B (+58% YoY)**, supply tight through 2027. [3-0, attributed forecast]
- **BofA structural logic:** 2030 AI-DC-systems TAM **~$1.7T** (up from $1.4T); HBM **$168B by 2030** (from
  $35B 2025); memory/accelerator **187GB→464GB**; "HBM ships with every AI accelerator." [3-0, forecast]
- **Micron (CEO Mehrotra):** completed price+volume agreements for its **entire CY2026 HBM supply**; HBM
  TAM **~40% CAGR, $35B→$100B by 2028** (pulled 2yr forward); supply "**substantially short of demand for
  the foreseeable future**" (~50–66% of key-customer demand met). [3-0, management/forecast]
- **SK Hynix (Oct 29 2025):** **sold out** entire DRAM, NAND, AND HBM for 2026; customers reserved slots.
  *(Speaker = head of DRAM marketing; "sold out" = bookings/LTAs, not delivered.)* [3-0]
- **Mechanism (corroborated even when searching to refute):** wafer reallocation from commodity DRAM →
  HBM (HBM ~3–4× wafer area/bit) shrinks commodity bit-supply → multi-year catch-up lag; prices forecast
  elevated through **2027–2028**. [3-0; lag duration is forecast]

### The "this time isn't different" rebuttal
- **Base-rate / dot-com parallel:** a June-2026 analysis explicitly maps the current "AI supercycle / new
  era" onto the failed late-1990s "**New Economy**" claim that tech had abolished the business cycle
  (Nasdaq +400% 1995→2000, then collapse). [3-0, single op-ed]
- **Cycle not structurally repealed:** memory remains a capital-intensive supply industry; simultaneous
  new-fab startups + slowing hyperscaler capex "**could quickly enter another phase of severe oversupply**."
  Even bulls concede "**supply response is inevitable**"; MS's supercycle flip called "analyst
  capitulation… historically a **mid-cycle signal**." [3-0]

### The most defensible synthesis + the killer nuance
- **ClearBridge:** semis "historically volatile/cyclical," but GenAI "**could elongate and reduce the
  severity** of the traditional boom/bust cycle" — retains cyclical verbs ("HBM demand eventually cools,"
  "eventually reach equilibrium"). Super-cycle through 2026 into 2027. ⇒ **dampened, not dead.** [3-0]
- **⭐ The inversion (most important nuance, independently corroborated):** **HBM margins are currently
  running BELOW commodity DRAM margins.** HBM is repriced on **annual** contracts (not multi-year-locked), set before the commodity spot spike;
  commodity DRAM reprices with spot/contract. TrendForce: **DDR5 64GB RDIMM surpassed HBM in per-wafer
  revenue *and* profitability in Q1 2026** (secondary coverage, not TrendForce, dubbed this a "historic first"); HBM3e premium over DDR5 compressed from
  ~4–5× toward ~1–2×; HBM contract prices shifting to YoY *decline* amid three-supplier competition while
  DDR5 contract prices keep rising. **Implication: HBM's stickiness protects the downside but caps the
  upside** (dampens its own cycle both ways); commodity DRAM stays the free-moving, more-cyclical segment. [3-0]

## Caveats
- **Epistemic asymmetry (the headline caveat):** bear case = realized audited fact; bull case = forecasts
  + vendors/sell-side talking their book, untested through a downturn. Weight accordingly.
- **Bull-side over-sourced:** explicit cautious-bear desk calls (MS peak indicator; a TechInsights 2027
  downturn) were referenced but **not verified standalone** — the body skews bull.
- **Source thinness:** the dot-com base-rate anchor is a single op-ed; some BofA figures trace a vendor
  newsroom (partly de-circularized via Futu/Moomoo) + a paywalled note; ClearBridge quotes via search
  extraction (JS-rendered page).
- **Forecast vs fact:** FY2023 losses are fact; everything about 2026–2030 (supercycle, TAMs, elevated
  prices, dampening) is forecast.

## Open questions
1. Has the cycle's **amplitude/frequency measurably changed** across multiple cycles, or is "dampening"
   still only narrative? (No rigorous multi-cycle time-series surfaced.)
2. **Verifiable HBM market-share structure** + concrete supply/capex-discipline evidence (the consolidation
   pillar's numbers were refuted).
3. The explicit **cautious-bear** position (MS peak indicator, TechInsights 2027 downturn, 2028–29 glut).
4. China **CXMT/YMTC** commodity threat size/timing; the **HBM4-oversupply-once-all-qualified** trigger.
5. Will HBM LTA pricing **protect margins through a downturn**, or is the current inversion a margin *drag*
   that also caps upside as three-supplier competition intensifies into 2027?
