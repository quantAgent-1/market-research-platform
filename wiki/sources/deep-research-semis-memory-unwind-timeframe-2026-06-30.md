## Bottom line — the duration estimate

The post-Micron memory/semis sell-off is a **flows-driven unwind**, and live positioning bounds its
**acute mechanical phase at ~2 weeks**, with the **bulk of the calendar-rebalancing pressure clearing
at/around the June 30 quarter-end close** — *not* persisting as a July overhang. Behind that sits a
**slower hedge-fund de-gross / vol-control re-leverage tail of a further ~1–2 weeks into mid-July.**
The **single biggest conditioning variable is the July 2 US jobs print**: a hot read could push price
through the next CTA trigger (~3% lower S&P / ~5% NDX from mid-June) and **re-accelerate the
systematic leg**.

| Phase | Window | Driver | Cadence read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calendar rebalancing** | discrete June closes — **Jun 26 Russell recon (done)**, **Jun 30 quarter-end** | pensions/SWFs immunizing; index recon | **clears in-window** at quarter-end (a June event, not a July overhang) |
| **CTA / systematic break** | front-loaded once a price **threshold** trips (breached Jun 23) | trend de-leveraging, sell>buy asymmetry in a down tape | **violent at the front end**, a handful of sessions to ~2 wks |
| **Levered-ETF de-lever** | intraday/days | daily constant-leverage rebalance = synthetic short gamma | **fastest** — amplifies the front, not a tail |
| **HF de-gross tail** | **weeks**, into ~mid-July | discretionary trimming of longs | the **slow tail** behind the mechanical legs |
| **Vol-control re-leverage** | vol-gated (no fixed clock) | rebuild as realized vol decays | a *bullish* tell when it turns, not a selling clock |

**Net:** acute forced-flow phase **~2 weeks** (bulk at/around Jun 30) **+ a ~1–2 week de-gross/vol-control
tail into mid-July**, conditioned on Jul-2 jobs. Confidence **medium** — the per-flow cadences are
individually grounded, but summing them to a session count is a synthesis estimate, not a measurement.

## Per-cohort verified ledger (vote = adversarial refute-attempts survived)

### 1. Quarter-end rebalancing — pensions / SWFs + index recon · **HIGH** · *current*
The selling is **concentrated into discrete June calendar closes and largely clears by quarter-end —
a June-window event, not a July overhang.**
- **JPMorgan ~$165B** June quarter-end equity selling "before June closes" (3-0).
- **Top-100 US pensions a record ~110% funded** (highest since 2001; Milliman 109.6% at May 31) →
  **"de-gliding and immunizing portfolios, creating a mechanical source of equity selling into
  month-end"** (Citadel) (3-0).
- **Russell recon** took effect at the **Jun 26 close** (record $553.9B cross), coinciding with MU's
  −6.7% that session (2-1).
- **⚠️ Do NOT assert:** that a forced **MU Russell Value→Growth** rebalance *caused* the −6.7%
  (**REFUTED 0-3** — the migration is *two-sided*, net direction ambiguous, layered on the all-day
  hot-PCE + IV-crush move); that **July 1 cleanly flips** quarter-end selling to net buying
  (**REFUTED 1-2** — no clean bullish reversal at the turn; de-gliding logically *recurs* into the
  Jul 31 month-end).

### 2. CTA / systematic trend — **HIGH** · *stale levels, durable mechanic*
Entered the drawdown **crowded near multi-month/multi-year highs** — a **"loaded spring"** with little
room to add and large **downside-asymmetric** selling capacity.
- Mid-May Citadel: CTA Nasdaq exposure **highest since October**, S&P **highest since November**,
  Russell 2000 **highest since Dec 2020** (3-0).
- Goldman **Jun 2**: CTAs **~$93B long global equities** (~$34B S&P futures), still adding ~$3B/wk (3-0).
- **BofA Jun 14**: next systematic-sell trigger at **~3% lower S&P / ~5% NDX** — a **threshold (not
  time) trigger** the Jun 23 flush breached (3-0).
- **Core shape mechanic:** "projected sell pressure in a down tape now materially exceeds incremental
  buy demand" → down days force more selling than up days force buying → **front-loaded, self-reinforcing,
  violent-not-slow-drift** (3-0).
- **⚠️ Do NOT cite** the **">$100B CTA selling"** downside magnitude (**REFUTED 0-3**) — use the
  asymmetry *direction*, not the figure. **Staleness:** all exposure levels are pre-unwind; **much may
  already have discharged in the Jun 23 flush** — the durable takeaways are positioning *extremity* and
  the *threshold/asymmetry* mechanic, not a live level.

### 3. Hedge-fund de-grossing — **HIGH** · *stale levels, multi-week cadence is the point*
Discretionary de-grossing runs on a **multi-week cadence ("weeks," not days)**, and **semiconductors
were the single most aggressively net-sold US subsector** heading in — via **trimming existing longs,
not initiating fresh shorts.**
- **GS Prime (~May 21):** semis & semicap the "most aggressively net-sold US subsector" over the
  trailing four weeks; selling = reducing longs, not new shorts (3-0).
- **GS Prime (early May):** "largest reduction in IT stock positions in a decade," long sales + short
  covers for a **"second consecutive week"** — an existence-proof that such de-grosses run **2+ weeks**
  (2-1).
- **GS Prime (Apr 27):** gross exposure collapsed **100th→3rd percentile "within weeks"** (3-0).
- **Cadence read:** this cohort is the **slower TAIL** of the unwind (the multi-week leg behind the
  single-session rebalancing and the front-loaded CTA break). **Staleness:** Apr–May snapshots
  (~5–8 wks pre-Micron), used as a *cadence prior* only — no verified late-June semis-specific de-gross
  magnitude exists.

### 4. Vol-control / risk-parity — **MEDIUM** · *mechanism sound, size debated*
**Procyclical** — cut leverage as realized vol rises → **mechanically reinforced (amplified) the flush**;
re-leveraging is **gated by realized vol normalizing, with no clean fixed calendar clock.**
- **ECB FSR (primary):** "fund managers have to liquidate leveraged positions when market volatility and
  cross-asset correlations surge, thereby reinforcing the selling pressure" (cites risk-parity sales
  ~225% of portfolio capital in March 2020) (3-0).
- **Duration implication:** re-allocation resumes only as realized vol decays → a falsifiable
  **tail-ending tell** is realized-vol normalization + VIX term-structure **re-contango**.
- **⚠️ Do NOT assert** a fixed **~1-month vol-control re-leverage clock** (**REFUTED 0-3** —
  vol-conditional, not calendar). **Magnitude debated** (AQR/Man Group: vol-control AUM may be too small
  to move markets materially) — direction sound, size uncertain.

### 5. Levered / single-stock ETFs — **HIGH** · *current* · **THE AMPLIFIER**
Record crowding acts as a **standing source of synthetic short gamma** whose **daily constant-leverage
rebalancing mechanically AMPLIFIES (does not dampen) the flush** — the fuel the Jun 23–24 Micron event
ignited.
- **Citadel July GMI:** levered/single-stock ETF AUM a record **~$218B mid-June** (+$82B/+60% since
  end-March; tech-linked leverage +136%, **semiconductor-linked +175% / nearly tripled**) (3-0).
- **Citadel Flow Fragility (May 18):** US levered-ETF AUM ~$203B, ~92% equities, ~70% tech — "a
  meaningful source of synthetic short gamma… the same flows that mechanically supported momentum higher
  can also exacerbate downside moves" (3-0). (Mechanism: daily-rebalance constant-leverage = short gamma,
  Cheng-Madhavan 2009.)
- **Named instruments:** **SOXL** (~$19.7B AUM, +262% YTD per corroboration), **Roundhill 2× DRAM "RAM,"**
  Korean single-stock 2× funds incl. the **world's-largest SK Hynix 2× ETF ~$5.4B.**
- **Duration relevance:** this cohort **de-levers FAST/intraday by construction → front-loaded violence,
  not a long tail.** **Caveats:** the ~$218B is a likely-*global* measure ~10% above US-only ~$198B; the
  +60% is off an April capitulation low (don't overstate trajectory); much would already have de-levered
  by late June — it is the **pre/mid-event fuel level, not remaining exposure.**

### 6. Options / dealer-gamma / skew — **MEDIUM** · **THINNEST dimension**
Broad-market tail-hedging demand was **elevated at the unwind's onset**, but a **semis-specific
dealer-gamma read could NOT be verified.**
- **CBOE SKEW 141.85 on Jun 23** — upper-decile (mean ~118–120); a valid **event-onset** baseline, not
  a current read (2-1). *(SKEW is a broad S&P gauge, not semis-specific.)*
- **⚠️ Unresolved / do NOT assert:** that dealers were **LONG gamma in semis** with an eroding cushion
  (**REFUTED 0-3**) → whether dealers now **amplify vs dampen** the semis complex is **UNRESOLVED**; the
  **Jun-23 VIX-backwardation** snapshot (**REFUTED 1-2**). No verified late-June semis IV-rank, put/call,
  VIX term structure, or COT.

## Falsifiable tells (what would confirm each path)
- **Rebalancing was dominant (clears in-window):** stabilization/bounce *after* the Jun 30 close.
- **Slower de-gross/vol-control tail still running:** selling persists into Jul 1–3 with *no* new catalyst.
- **Systematic leg re-accelerates:** a **hot Jul-2 jobs print** breaching the BofA ~3% S&P / ~5% NDX trigger.
- **Tail ending / vol-control rebuilding:** realized-vol decay + **VIX re-contango**.

## Caveats & limitations — staleness is the dominant caveat
The actual exposure **LEVELS** for nearly every cohort are **pre-unwind snapshots**, so the analysis
rests on **cadence/mechanism (which decay slowly)** far more than verified live magnitude. Dating ledger:
- **STALE:** CTA positioning extremes + levered-ETF synthetic-short-gamma read = **mid-May** (~6 wks);
  GS Prime de-grossing = **Apr 27–May 21** (~5–8 wks); Goldman April pension figure = Apr 27.
- **SEMI-CURRENT:** Goldman CTA $93B long = **Jun 2**; BofA ~3% trigger = **Jun 14**; CBOE SKEW = **Jun 23**.
- **CURRENT (load-bearing):** Citadel ~$218B levered-ETF AUM; JPMorgan ~$165B quarter-end; Russell timing;
  Citadel's "2-week mechanics window."
- **The largest single unknown:** how much of the May-dated CTA max-long and Apr–May de-gross dry powder
  was **already discharged in the Jun 23 flush** vs. still remains to sell — this directly sets the
  tail's length.
- **Refuted sub-claims that must NOT be used** (collected): clean Jul-1 flip to buying (1-2); dealers
  long-gamma in semis (0-3); fixed ~1-month vol-control re-leverage clock (0-3); ">$100B CTA selling"
  magnitude (0-3); Jun-23 VIX backwardation (1-2); standalone "forced MU Russell rebalance caused −6.7%"
  (0-3). One off-regime source (ZeroHedge/McElligott, May 7 — a *melt-up*, not this selloff) was excluded.
- The aggregate **duration is a synthesis estimate, not a measurement**, conditioned heavily on Jul-2 jobs.
