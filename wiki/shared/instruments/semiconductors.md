# Semiconductors (Sector)

The semiconductor sector (SOX/PHLX index; SMH and SOXX ETFs; megacaps NVDA, AMD, AVGO, TSM, MU, ASML)
is the leadership group of the 2023-2026 AI rally and the worked example for
[swing trading](../../systematic-trading/strategies/swing-trading.md) a hot regime.

## Durable structural traits
- **High beta & deep cyclicality** — the "SOX cycle" tracks the capex / inventory cycle; the sector
  routinely moves far more than the market both ways.
- **Severe historical drawdowns** — dot-com ~−80%+, plus ~−35% in 2018 and 2022. Concentration in a
  few megacaps + earnings-gap risk (NVDA shed ~$600B in a *single day*, Jan 2025).

## The regime, with data (mid-2026 — time-sensitive)
- **2023-25 was NVDA-led:** NVDA +239% (2023), +171% (2024), +39% (2025) drove the tape.
- **April 2026 — leadership broadened sharply:** SOXX +40.4% (largest month in its 25-yr history),
  SMH +32.2% (best ever) — yet **NVDA was the *slowest* mega-cap chipmaker (+21.9%)** while Intel
  (+119%), Credo (+88%), Astera Labs (+84%), AMD (+75%), Marvell (+65%) and Micron (+55%) led across
  networking, memory/HBM, and legacy names — a near-complete *inversion* of the 2024-25 leaderboard
  (Micron ~+762% TTM, joined the $1T club). SMH's ~18% NVDA weight dragged it vs SOXX's ~7%.
- **Why it matters:** a broadening, rotating tape changes which names "work" — and a leadership
  handoff *away from the generals* is historically a **late-stage** signal worth watching, not
  celebrating uncritically.

## Memory & HBM — the 2026 sub-cycle (see Market Research)
The memory names (MU, Samsung, SK Hynix) are in a distinct **HBM-led "supercycle"**: HBM is sold out
for 2026 and **crowds out commodity DRAM** (it eats ~3–4× the wafer area per GB), keeping commodity
DRAM/NAND tight even with mild PC/phone demand. The bifurcation matters — **HBM is expected to stay
tight into 2027** while any oversupply risk concentrates in **commodity DRAM** (Big-Three + China/CXMT
capacity). The near-term catalyst is **Micron's FQ3-2026 print (June 24, 2026)** into an all-time-high,
+756%/yr run — a classic sell-the-news setup (cf. AVGO's ~14% post-beat drop, June 3). Full detail in
market research: [memory supply-demand cycle](../../market-research/memory/supply-demand-cycle-2026.md)
· [Micron FQ3-2026 earnings](../../market-research/memory/micron-fq3-2026-earnings.md) · [SK Hynix](../../market-research/memory/sk-hynix.md) · [cyclical vs "new era" verdict](../../market-research/memory/cyclical-vs-new-era.md).

## Real driver or bubble? (both sides evidenced)
- **Bull (verified):** NVDA Q3 FY2026 revenue **$57.0B (+62% YoY)**, Data Center **$51.2B (+66%)** ≈
  90% of revenue, Q4 guide **$65.0B** — a genuine earnings step-change (GAAP). Big-Five hyperscaler
  capex ~$256B→$443B→$602B (2024→26). *(Scope note, lint 2026-07-02: this **Big-Five** figure sits
  below the H2 playbook's "$725B 2026"
  — a broader, less-verified "all hyperscalers" bucket; unreconciled, neither page defines its
  issuer set precisely.)*
- **Bear (contested):** Michael Burry (Nov 2025) accused hyperscalers of **understating depreciation**
  (extending chip useful-life), ~$176B 2026-28, and disclosed bearish puts — the dot-com parallel +
  ~$108B/yr hyperscaler debt / overbuild risk. *Disputed (Nvidia rebutted); what's established is
  that he made the claim.*
- **Signals the regime is turning:** capex-guidance cuts, GPU/HBM order softening, TSMC utilization
  rolling over, depreciation-schedule restatements, or SOX/SMH breaking the 200-day MA.
