## Summary
The central question was: into H2 2026 (Jul–Dec), is the memory cycle still expanding (hold/press) or peaking
(distribute)? **As of print day the answer is unambiguously *still expanding*, on every observable tripwire.**
Forward EPS estimates are being revised **sharply up** (the master signal); DRAM/NAND contract prices were
**still rising month/month into 3Q26**; the supply deficit is the **worst in 15 years and widening into 2027**;
inventories sit at **2–4 weeks vs 15–31 weeks at prior cycle peaks**; CY2026 HBM is **sold out under multi-year
LTAs**; and the cycle-ending oversupply signal (new-fab output, a CXMT flood) is dated to **late 2027/2028, not
visible mid-2026.** The bull case has matured into an explicit **de-cyclicalization** argument (memory reframed
as AI infrastructure, not a commodity), carrying MU targets to **$1,200–1,625**. The honest counter is that
de-cyclicalization is **unproven** (memory has never broken its boom-bust pattern) and a price/EPS *peak* can
arrive on **demand digestion or estimate-revision rollover well before** physical oversupply — so "supply is
tight" must **not** be read as "safe to hold indefinitely." The load-bearing forward catalyst specific to Micron
is **2027 HBM allocation**: it is winning the cycle but **losing the HBM share race** (SK Hynix ~62% vs Micron
~18–21%).

## Key takeaways (verified findings)
1. **Cycle state — still expanding (high confidence).** 2Q26 conventional **DRAM +58–63% QoQ** (decelerating from
   a record +90–95% in 1Q26), **NAND +70–75% QoQ and still *accelerating***; even legacy DDR2 guided **+55–60%
   (2Q) → +35–40% (3Q26)**. TrendForce (Jun 22, 2026): prices "expected to continue rising in 3Q26." No source
   shows any price *falling*. *Nuance:* DRAM's peak **rate-of-change** (2nd derivative) is likely behind us; the
   *level* is still rising.
2. **Forward EPS-revision direction = UP (the master tripwire, high confidence).** FY2026 consensus EPS **~$57.71**,
   FY2027 **~$97.77** → **~10× forward** at $950–1,050. Q3 consensus EPS revised **+3.1% (30d), +68% (90d)**.
   Targets tripled within a month: **UBS $535→$1,625, RBC $525→$1,200, Rosenblatt $600→$1,200, BofA $950→$1,500
   (Jun 23)**. A targeted adversarial search for *down*-revisions returned only up-revisions. *(Caveat carried from
   the [earnings page](../market-research/memory/micron-fq3-2026-earnings.md): part of the 90-day jump is mechanical
   catch-up to Micron's own raised guidance, not pure new bullishness.)*
3. **Micron earnings momentum (high).** Q2 FY26 set records — **$23.9B rev (+196% YoY)**, DRAM $18.8B (+207%),
   NAND $5.0B (+169%), ~75% margins. Q3 FY26 (reported Jun 24) consensus **$34.4–35.6B rev, ~81.6% record gross
   margin, EPS ~$19.72 (+932% YoY)** — **above** management's own guidance ($33.5B / $18.90). Consensus exceeding
   guidance = estimates still chasing the company up.
4. **HBM4 / NVIDIA integration (high).** Micron began **HBM4 36GB 12-high volume shipment in CQ1 2026** for
   NVIDIA's **Vera Rubin** (>2.8 TB/s, >20% better power); declared "high-volume production" Mar 16. On Jun 5 in
   Seoul, Jensen Huang confirmed **all three suppliers qualified and shipping** HBM4 for Vera Rubin.
5. **Demand is structural, not speculative (high).** The price strength is **physical wafer reallocation** —
   HBM is ~3× more wafer-intensive per GB; HBM now ~23% of DRAM wafers (up from ~19% in 2025), starving commodity
   bits; DDR4 output cut to ~20% of prior with prices +170%. NA cloud providers buying high-capacity RDIMMs for
   inference; NAND directed to enterprise SSDs (clear shortage).
6. **De-cyclicalization bull thesis & targets (high that it's *being argued*; the thesis itself is unproven).**
   BofA's Arya: DRAM/HBM "are not cyclical commodities… they are mission-critical AI infrastructure." BofA 2030:
   total semis **$2.7T** (from $2.3T), **memory ~$220B (2025) → ~$1.65T (2030, ~50% CAGR)**, **HBM $35B → $246B**.
   This is the math under the $1,500–1,625 targets — and the **exact assumption a bear disputes.**
7. **What ends the cycle is NOT visible mid-2026 (high).** Micron can fill only **50%–two-thirds** of demand
   (CEO); new fabs (Idaho first wafers mid-CY2027, Singapore meaningful FY2028) don't add volume before 2028; NAND
   capex growing only ~5%. **Goldman 2026 DRAM supply gap 4.9%** (worst in 15 yrs; NAND 4.2%, HBM 5.1%), **widening
   to ~5.9% in 2027**; CY2026 memory fully contracted via LTAs incl. a new **Anthropic** deal (2–3yr visibility).
   *Logical caveat: supply relief being years away does NOT mean the price/EPS peak is years away.*
8. **Micron's key forward catalyst = 2027 HBM share (high).** CY2026 sold out, so the stock now trades on **2027
   allocation**. **SK Hynix ~62% HBM share** (~54–70% of NVIDIA HBM4); **Micron only ~18–21%**, the smallest Vera
   Rubin supplier, Korean rivals "widening their lead." Micron is **winning the cycle but losing the share race**.
9. **CXMT/China is a 2027–28 risk, not a 2026 breaker (high).** SemiAnalysis (Jun 23): DRAM **undersupplied** high-
   single-digit % in 2026 → low-to-mid-teens in 2027; CXMT **bit**-share only **9% (2025) → 12% (2027)** — gradual,
   not a near-term flood. Every glut scenario found dates the turn to **2027–2029**.
10. **Exit-signal monitoring checklist (medium — a synthesis).** Tripwires for the roll-over: (1) forward EPS
    revisions flatten/roll *(primary; currently UP)*; (2) contract prices m/m stall→negative *(currently rising)*;
    (3) inventory build above normal *(currently 2–4 wks vs 15–31 at peaks)*; (4) hyperscaler capex guidance cuts;
    (5) supplier capex / new-fab output catching demand *(not before late-2027/28)*; (6) CXMT share inflecting past
    9%→12%. Room-to-run tells: consensus printing above guidance, LTAs extending into 2027/28, the gap **widening**.
