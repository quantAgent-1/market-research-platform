# Trade Checklist — Catching the V-Day (Buying a Constraint-Driven Flush)

> Education, **not investment advice.** Run this when a crowded complex you know well (the home case:
> KR memory/semis) has fallen hard for 2+ sessions **without a fundamental print scaled to the move**,
> and you suspect the fall is flow. It exists to convert "I expected a bounce but didn't know when"
> into a scheduled, staffed, falsifiable trade. **No item here calls the bottom.** The method is
> condition-recognition + trigger-confirmation: you buy the *confirmation* of exhaustion and accept
> paying a few percent off the exact low for it. Anatomy in full:
> [liquidity cascades & V-reversals](../../shared/concepts/liquidity-cascades-and-v-reversals.md) ·
> Korea-specific machinery: [KRX session clocks & forced liquidation](../../shared/concepts/krx-session-clocks-and-forced-liquidation.md).

## The four claims, separated (know which one you're betting on)

The trade bundles four predictions with different evidence classes and different confidence. Keep
them separate — most mistakes come from letting confidence in one leak into another.

| Claim | Evidence class | Confidence available ex ante |
|---|---|---|
| **Direction** — it reverts | First principles: breadth + no news + nameable constrained sellers | High — this is the strongest leg |
| **Day** — the low prints *this* session | Calendar arithmetic: the forced-sale conveyor belt + fuel gauge | Moderate — "candidate day," not certainty |
| **Minute** — the turn comes ~10:00 KST | Session clocks: forced supply exhausts · algos rotate · Taiwan opens | A *window to staff*, never a forecast |
| **Magnitude** — violent, ~mirror of the fall | Impact symmetry on a still-thin book | Distributional only — never a point estimate |

## The checklist

### ☐ 1 · Condition — is this flow, not information?

All four must lean "flow." One clear "information" answer kills the trade.

- [ ] **Breadth:** is the *whole* complex down in lockstep? Name-specific private information doesn't
  hit six names at once ([informed vs. uninformed flow](../../shared/concepts/informed-vs-uninformed-flow.md)).
- [ ] **Damage ordered by crowdedness, not by news:** are the most-crowded names falling hardest,
  independent of their news exposure? An unwind sorts casualties by popularity; information doesn't.
- [ ] **No fundamental print scaled to the move:** no guidance cut, no estimate revisions down, no
  demand-crack datapoint. Commentary and think-pieces don't count; cash-flow news does.
- [ ] **Nameable constrained sellers:** can you say *who* must sell regardless of price (foreign
  de-grossing streak, margin conveyor, leveraged-ETF rebalance)? If you can't name the payer, pass
  ([who pays you](../../shared/concepts/who-pays-you.md)).

### ☐ 2 · Fuel gauge — was the leverage there to cascade?

A violent flush needs pre-positioned forced sellers. Check the stock of fuel *before* trusting the
cascade read:

- [ ] **Margin credit balance (신용융자 잔고) percentile** — KOFIA publishes it
  [daily and free](https://freesis.kofia.or.kr/stat/FreeSIS.do?parentDivId=MSIS10000000000000&serviceId=STATSCU0100000070).
  At the July 2026 episode it sat at an all-time high (₩38.6T, +₩11T in six months).
- [ ] **Concentrated in the falling names?** Fuel in *these* names (and their single-stock leveraged
  ETFs) is what converts a dip into a conveyor-belt cascade.
- [ ] **The mechanism already visibly firing:** 반대매매 headlines, FSS/broker risk chatter,
  pre-open limit-down indications. (In June–July 2026: an FSS CRO meeting on forced-liquidation risk
  nine days before the low, and "빚투 공포" front pages on day 2.)

### ☐ 3 · Calendar — is the belt loaded for tomorrow morning?

The Korea-specific edge: margin shortfalls are measured at the **close** and force-sold at the
**next open** (D/D+1/D+2; below 120% fast-tracks to one day). So count closes, not lows:

- [ ] **Two+ consecutive hard down closes** → breach lists stacked, fast-track accounts queued →
  the *next morning* is the scheduled capitulation slot.
- [ ] **Sell sidecar on day 2?** Historically a sell-sidecar day on no fundamental news resolves to
  a buy sidecar within a session or two (Aug 5→6 2024 · Mar 4→5 2026 · the 2008 and 2020 clusters).
- [ ] **Unsustainable slope:** a −20%+/2-day move in index heavyweights is near the edge of what any
  seller can physically execute — selling at that participation rate exhausts itself.
- [ ] **The mirror rule:** a session that crashes intraday but *closes recovered* loads nothing —
  don't expect a next-morning flush after a saved close.

### ☐ 4 · The 9:00–10:30 trigger sequence — staff the window, buy the confirmation

Run these in order on the candidate morning. Entries staged (halves/thirds), first tranche only
after the exhaustion tells, not into the falling knife:

- [ ] **8:30–9:00 — pre-open auction indicatives:** limit-down-ish indications on no news = the
  queued forced supply, visible before the bell.
- [ ] **9:00–9:45 — let the belt empty:** a gap-up that *fails* means forced supply is still
  clearing (it sells regardless of the bounce). Wait for the exhaustion tape: climax volume at the
  extreme → a failed push to new lows → the **first higher low**.
- [ ] **~10:00 — the clocks:** Taiwan/TSMC's opening print (does it refute the information story?);
  KRX investor-type flow tape — **institutional net buying turning positive while price is still
  down** is the single best real-time confirmation.
- [ ] **10:00–10:30 — hold test:** futures leading cash, basis normalizing, the morning low intact.
  If yes → the constraint is spent; add the second tranche.
- [ ] **Accept the toll:** confirmation entry costs ~3–5% vs the print low. The edge never required
  catching the low — on Jul 3 2026 a 10:15 entry still captured ~12–15% by the close.

### ☐ 5 · Falsifiers — pre-commit them before the first tranche

Write these down before entering. Any one fires → exit, no renegotiation
([navigating nonlinear markets](../../synthesis/navigating-nonlinear-markets.md)):

- [ ] **A fundamental print scaled to the move** (estimate cuts, guidance warning) → it was
  information; a genuine demand crack does not V.
- [ ] **New lows after ~10:30 on rising volume** → the constraint is bigger than Seoul's morning
  (the Aug-5-2024 pattern: global carry unwind, no 10:00 rescue, −8% close).
- [ ] **Taiwan opens in freefall** → the 10:00 clock is *confirming* the information story.
- [ ] **A weak close after the bounce** → tonight's close reloads the belt for tomorrow; the
  multi-day-constraint variant is in play (late-June 2026: a ~two-week acute phase). Exit or cut;
  re-run the checklist tomorrow.

### ☐ 6 · Sizing & exit discipline

- [ ] **Size for the torpedo case.** Sometimes it *was* information and doesn't come back — the
  compensation exists because of this tail. Survivable-size first
  ([risk of ruin](../../shared/concepts/risk-of-ruin.md)); sizing problem, not conviction problem.
- [ ] **No tight price stop inside the flush** — stops in a mean-reverting regime are engineered to
  sell the bottom ([stop-losses & exits](../../shared/concepts/stop-losses-and-exits.md)). The exit
  is the falsifier list above, not a percent.
- [ ] **Never stand on the forced side yourself:** no margin, no leveraged vehicle that rebalances
  against you. The cascade is a flow you harvest, never one you feed.
- [ ] **Take-profit logic:** the V recovers the *temporary* component; the crowding-premium part of
  the fall may stay repriced. Expect "the flush plus a chunk of the prior days," not a full
  round-trip to the old high (Jul 3 2026: +9.7% on the day ≈ the morning flush + roughly a third of
  the two-day damage).

### ☐ 7 · After — journal it

- [ ] **Which of the four claims was right/wrong, separately?** (Direction/day/minute/magnitude —
  grade each.) A win on the wrong reasoning is a loss in disguise.
- [ ] **Did the close cure the belt?** (Strong up-close = chain broken; weak = expect a reload.)
- [ ] File the episode: date, fuel percentile, down-close count, trigger times, entry toll vs the
  low — this is exactly the episode-library input the engine's H6 module consumes.

## Worked example — July 1–3, 2026 (the page's origin)

| Jul 2 | SKH −14%, Samsung −9%; KOSPI −5% intraday, **sell sidecar** (30th of 2026); "반대매매 3조" front pages | Belt maximally loaded; next morning = scheduled capitulation slot |
