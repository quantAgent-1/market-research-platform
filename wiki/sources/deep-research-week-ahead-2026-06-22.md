## Verified backbone

### The Fed — hawkish backdrop (settled the week before; not a live event this week)
- **FOMC June 16-17 held the funds rate at 3.50-3.75%** (unanimous 12-0; 4th straight hold; range since
  Dec 2025). Next FOMC: **July 28-29**. [3-0, Fed primary]
- **The dot plot turned HAWKISH:** median end-2026 dot **3.8%** (from 3.4% in March); **9 of 18 project
  ≥1 hike, only 1 a cut**, 6 see ~two hikes. The Fed **raised 2026 inflation forecasts to 3.6% headline /
  3.3% core PCE** (from 2.7%/2.7%). [3-0, Fed SEP primary]
- **Market-implied next move = hike-or-hold, NOT a cut** — futures repriced a possible hike **as early as
  October**; CME FedWatch ~**36% odds of a 25bp hike / ~34% of 50bp** before year-end, ~14% no-change;
  StockTitan ~**77% odds of a hike by December** (from ~24% a month earlier). [3-0]
- **Kevin Warsh's FIRST meeting as the 17th Fed Chair** (succeeded Powell ~May 22 2026) — underpins the
  "higher-for-longer / possible hikes" pivot (he declined to submit a dot, hence 18 of 19). [3-0]

### The calendar — Thursday June 25 is the loaded morning
- **PCE Price Index (Personal Income & Outlays, May) AND the final Q1-2026 GDP both release Thu June 25,
  8:30 ET** — together, NOT Friday June 26 (corrects the common assumption). Weekly jobless claims also
  Thursday. [3-0, BEA primary]
- **Core PCE consensus 0.2% MoM / 3.3% YoY; headline PCE ~4.0% YoY** (Trading Economics, secondary). April
  was **3.3% core** (hottest since Nov 2023, BEA-confirmed). *(A stray 2.6% core consensus was judged
  implausible; the GDP 1.6% consensus was refuted — only the date is firm there.)* [3-0 medium]
- **Into a hike-leaning Fed, a HOT Core PCE (≥0.3% MoM) is the week's single biggest macro risk.**
