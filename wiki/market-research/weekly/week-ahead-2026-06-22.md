## One-line setup
**A hawkish-Fed-dominated week with two stacked catalysts — Micron Wednesday night and PCE Thursday
morning — into a stretched, low-volatility, holiday-shortened tape, plus an Iran/oil tail risk.** The
dominant new variable is the **Fed**, not the data calendar.

## ✅ The backbone (verified)
- **The Fed turned hawkish under a new Chair.** The June 16–17 FOMC (the *prior* week) held at
  **3.50–3.75%** (unanimous), but the **dot plot flipped to HIKES** — median end-2026 dot **3.8%** (from
  3.4%), **9 of 18 see ≥1 hike, only 1 a cut**; 2026 inflation forecast raised to **3.6% headline / 3.3%
  core**. **Kevin Warsh's first meeting as the 17th Fed Chair.** Markets now price a **possible hike by
  Oct/Dec (FedWatch ~36% 25bp / ~34% 50bp), not a cut.** → a headwind for the stretched AI/long-duration trade.
- **Thursday 6/25, 8:30 ET = the loaded morning: PCE *and* final Q1 GDP together** (not Friday). Core PCE
  consensus **0.2% MoM / 3.3% YoY**, headline ~4.0%; April ran 3.3% (hottest since Nov '23). **Into a
  hike-leaning Fed, a hot PCE (≥0.3% MoM) is the week's biggest macro risk.**
