## The verdict — by the three senses of "re-rate"
A "re-rate" is the **multiple** moving, not the earnings (P = E × multiple). Split into three, the verified
evidence lands on the **same conclusion the prior wiki reached — and now measures it**:

1. **EXPANSION (is the market paying more per dollar?) — REAL, but exactly one cycle old and born at the
   peak.** SK Hynix's year-end P/B sat in a **flat 0.81-1.87 band across 2017-2024 with no secular drift**;
   the break to ~4-4.5× is a **2025-26 event** — i.e. the expansion arrived *at the cyclical top*, which is
   exactly what the "peak-cycle multiple expansion that reverses" bear thesis predicts.
2. **RE-CATEGORIZATION (to a genuine secular/premium multiple) — NOT yet awarded on the headline metric;
   the framework-switch is only beginning, and it's bull-led.** Memory still trades at cyclical forward
   multiples; even Morgan Stanley still values SK Hynix on an explicitly cyclical frame; the clearest
   contrary tell (one analyst literally switching Micron from peak-P/B to a growth P/E) is recent,
   Micron-only, and itself shaped like a late-cycle "analyst capitulation."
3. **CONFIRMATION (durability proven by surviving a downturn) — UNPROVEN, and the test window is now dated
   ~2027-28.** No downturn has tested the AI-era multiple; named bears + the base-rate pattern argue the
   "cycle is dead" claim is itself a classic top signal.

**Net: the market is paying up while pointedly *withholding the premium* — pricing a re-rate *probability*,
not a *fact*.**

## Confirmed findings

### Sense 1 — EXPANSION: now measured against a flat pre-AI baseline
- **SK Hynix year-end P/B, 2017→2024: 1.59 · 0.88 · 1.34 · 1.56 · 1.45 · 0.81 · 1.87 · 1.62** — a tight
  **0.81-1.87 band** in which *boom* year-ends (2017, 2020, 2023) and *bust* year-ends (2018, 2022) **both
  stayed sub-2×**, with **no secular drift**. The break to **~4-4.5× P/B in 2025** is therefore a brand-new
  2025-26 development — the expansion is real but **one cycle old, and it broke out at the peak**.
  [3-0; companiesmarketcap, corroborated by mlq.ai]
- **Trailing P/E is useless as a cross-cycle gauge for memory** — it *collapses to ~3-4× at peaks* (Micron
  3.10× at Nov-2018 on TTM EPS $12.13; 3.66× Feb-2019; 3.41× in 2010) because EPS peaks, and goes
  *negative/undefined at troughs* (Micron FY2023 −$5.34/sh, FY2012 −$1.04, FY2009 −$2.29 — losses every
  quarter). **Implication: a low trailing/forward multiple at a cyclical top is the NORM, not evidence of a
  re-rate** — you must read P/B + normalized EPS, exactly as the bear case insists.
  [3-0; macrotrends paywalled but every number cross-verified against **primary SEC filings**]

### Sense 2 — RE-CATEGORIZATION: genuine but incipient and bull-led
- **Still priced as cyclicals (no secular premium capitalized).** Late May 2026: **SK Hynix ~6.1× forward,
  Micron ~9.6× forward** — both far below the South-Korea semi industry (~23×) and the global semi median
  (~36×). [2-1; Benzinga May 27, corroborated by Simply Wall St / GuruFocus]
- **Even Morgan Stanley still uses a cyclical frame.** MS valued SK Hynix at **"5.3× expected 2026 earnings"**
  (₩840k target) and called it **"~4× peak earnings"**; its Sept-2025 note anchored to **"2.0× 2026e P/B
  (historical peak-cycle)"** + residual income — classic cyclical methods, no growth-premium DCF, even while
  raising targets. (And the price then ran *past* the targets — the same "price outruns cyclical-framed
  targets" dynamic seen at Micron.) [2-1; investing.com]
- **⭐ The clearest tell that re-categorization IS starting:** on **June 15, 2026, Aletheia Capital switched
  its Micron model from the historical peak-P/B method to a P/E on CY2027 estimates** (10× CY27 EPS, target
  $650→$1,600), writing that it now views Micron **"as a growth technology company, rather than continuing to
  frame it within a cyclical stock valuation system."** Same day: **TD Cowen $660→$1,500** ("memory in AI is
  structural rather than cyclical"), **RBC $525→$1,200** (~10× CY27 EPS) — hikes of **~+120-145%**. [3-0]
  *Bounding caveats:* Aletheia is a smaller Asia-focused, Buy-rated house **talking its book**; the CY27 EPS
  inputs are aggressive (HBM ASP doubling); it's **Micron-only**; and hikes this large *after* MU is **+224%
  YTD** are themselves the "analyst capitulation = late-cycle signal" pattern.
- **The bull side concedes the re-rate hasn't happened.** SK Hynix's own sell-side (Hana Securities, Rok-ho
  Kim, May 13 2026): to advance further the stock **"will need not only solid earnings but also a re-rating
  of its valuation multiple"** — i.e. framed as still-to-come, and earnings-alone-insufficient. [3-0; but
  source = SK Hynix's *own newsroom* (book-talking), so medium]
- **The re-categorization is openly CONTESTED** (CNBC, May 25 2026): named fund managers dispute it —
  **William de Gale (BlueBox)** ("whenever the argument emerges that the memory cycle is gone… it goes
  horribly wrong" — the claim is itself a top signal) and **Andrew Lapping (Ranmore)** ("a leopard does not
  often change its spots"; an industry of "historically mediocre return on capital… now expected to deliver
  extraordinary returns"). [3-0; skeptical journalism, the opposite of book-talking]

### Sense 3 — CONFIRMATION: unproven, test window ~2027-28
- **Willy Shih (Harvard Business School):** the current boom is **"the same cycle, except bigger amplitude"**
  and **"this too will pass"** — via the 60-year glut mechanism (capacity raced in at high prices arrives
  together, prices crash); new fab *volume* not until **late 2027-2028**. [3-0; Fortune May 11, fab timing
  corroborated]
- **Morgan Stanley (Sept 2025): supercycle peaks ~2027.** *Use as a timing marker only* — this is the
  conservative read of an *evolving bull* thesis (MS later turned more bullish), **not** a crash call. [3-0]
- **The Micron bear case is an explicit peak-cycle reversal** (glut if Samsung/SK Hynix close the HBM gap;
  capex squeezing margins; a downside scenario of **MU ~$260-300 by 2030 ≈ −75%** from ~$1,100). [3-0 on the
  framing; the $260-300 figure itself is a weak aggregator — treat as illustrative, not a forecast]

### Structural pillar — the load-bearing disconfirming fact
- **The HBM-margin-below-commodity-DDR5 inversion is CONFIRMED persistent through mid-2026, not a one-quarter
  artifact.** Per **TrendForce's primary press release (June 2, 2026):** *"HBM wafer revenue was overtaken by
  DDR5 64GB RDIMM in 1Q26. This has led HBM profitability to also fall below that of DDR5 64GB RDIMM since
  1Q26."* This is the **cleanest single argument against the secular story** — a real HBM re-rate should show
  as a premium *on the HBM segment*, yet HBM is currently the **lower-margin** product per wafer. [3-0,
  primary]
- **Honest counterweight (bull):** TrendForce calls the inversion **"unsustainable"** and forecasts **HBM
  contract prices to "surge multiples higher" in 2027** (tight DRAM supply, crowding-out, HBM4 complexity;
  corroborating low end ~2×). [3-0, primary] **But** a 2027 price spike that mean-reverts *is the cyclical
  thesis, not the secular one* — pricing power ≠ permanence.

### Behavioral / positioning + base rate
- **Framework migration is visibly happening but bull-led and narrow** — the +120-145% one-day Micron hikes
  (above) are the strongest behavioral tell, *and simultaneously* the classic late-cycle capitulation
  pattern. [3-0]
- **Foreign ownership of Samsung + SK Hynix hit a yearly LOW** (Sedaily, June 21) — the *opposite* of the
  "generalists rotating in" confirmation a durable re-rate would show. [in source set; folded as a tell]
- **Base rate sits AGAINST a durable re-categorization proven at a top:** "this time is different" /
  "cycle is dead" narratives cluster near cycle tops (de Gale, Shih; the late-1990s "New Economy" →
  dot-com bust; MS's supercycle conversion earlier called "analyst capitulation — historically a mid-cycle
  signal"). [medium; synthesis of 3-0 claims + prior wiki]
