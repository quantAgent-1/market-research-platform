- **Korean brokers cluster at ₩3.8M** — KB Securities (raised to ₩3.8M ~May 28-29, via ₩2.8M→₩3.0M),
  Korea Investment (May 20), Mirae Asset (₩3.2M→₩3.8M, May 27), Shinhan (₩1.9M→₩3.8M, May 21). **SK
  Securities ₩4.0M** (analyst Han Dong-hee, Jun 1, ~10× fwd P/E). Implied upside **~66%** to ₩3.8M,
  **~45%** to ₩4.0M. KB rationale (Kim Dong-won): "the pace of upward earnings revisions is outstripping
  the pace of share-price gains… ample room for a further re-rating." [3-0]
- **The Morgan Stanley "undervalued" lead = VERIFIED but STALE.** MS raised ₩730k→**₩840k** (Jan 2, 2026,
  Overweight, ~29% upside): "trading at about 4× peak earnings… little value is being assigned to HBM,"
  EPS forecasts +56%/+63%. Superseded by **₩1.1M** (Jan 30, "Memory, Double Up") — both now far below
  spot. Accurate as of date; do not present as MS's current view. [3-0 / 2-1]
- **Current price ~₩2,764,000** (Jun 19, +2.94%; intraday ATH ₩2.891M), mkt cap ~₩1,957.73T (~$1.28T);
  Jun 15 close ₩2.288M (+6.42%). [3-0]
- **Valuation:** forward P/E **7.64** vs trailing **26.16**; trailing EPS ₩105,641 (+185.7% YoY). The low
  forward multiple is **predicated on consensus EPS nearly tripling** — "cheap" only if the supercycle
  earnings are sustainable. [3-0]

### Market reality — HBM franchise (bull case, largely fact)
- **HBM leadership durable so far:** 62% shipment share (Q2'25), 57% revenue (Q3'25), 58% (Q1'26)
  [Counterpoint]; Goldman >50% through 2026; **world's first HBM4 mass-production system (Sept 2025)**;
  UBS ~70% of NVIDIA Rubin HBM4. Nuance: HBM4 volume ramp postponed Q2→Q3'26 to align with Rubin. [3-0]

### Market reality — financials & valuation
- **Record FY2025 (actuals):** revenue **₩97.15T (+47%)**, op profit **₩47.21T (+101%, 49% margin)**,
  net income **₩42.95T (+117%)**. Q4'25: revenue ₩32.83T, op profit ₩19.17T (**58% margin**), NI ₩15.25T.
  **Q1'26 op profit ₩37.61T (+405% YoY, ~72% margin).** HBM revenue >2× YoY (HBM-%-of-DRAM not disclosed). [3-0]

### Bear case — the one verified risk + the gap
- **HBM4 competition is broadening (the concrete verified risk).** **June 5, 2026: NVIDIA CEO Jensen
  Huang confirmed all three (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron) are qualified for Vera Rubin HBM4** ("All three
  vendors have been qualified"). Samsung began HBM4 shipments **Feb 2026** (~25-30%); Korea Herald
  (Dec'25) reported Samsung's HBM4 "delivered the strongest overall results" in NVIDIA testing, gap
  narrowing. SK Hynix still leads ~60-70%, but the near-monopoly has broadened. [3-0 / 2-1]

### Cycle backdrop
- 2026 HBM market **$54.6B, +58% YoY** (BofA; Goldman more conservative ~$45B); HBM3E ~2/3 of 2026 HBM
  shipments. [3-0, medium confidence — single primary IR page for the headline]

- **Forecast vs fact:** FY2025 financials, the Jun-5 Huang qualification statement, and daily prices are
  **facts**; all price targets, the $54.6B HBM TAM, the ~70% Rubin allocation, and forward P/E are
  **forecasts/estimates** (NVIDIA has not disclosed actual HBM4 allocation shares).
- **Source mix:** primary for financials/HBM share; secondary (Korean-press English + aggregators) for
  targets/prices, with wire corroboration. Two claims passed only 2-1 (MS Jan-2 target; Samsung Feb HBM4).
- **Time-sensitive:** intraday ATH ₩2.891M on Jun 19; price/cap/multiples/targets drift fast.
- **Bear-case gap:** CXMT, 2027 commodity-DRAM glut, NVIDIA customer-concentration, capex intensity,
  valuation stretch, and Korea-specific (won/governance/tax) risks did **NOT** survive as standalone
