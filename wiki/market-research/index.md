# Market Research

This area holds **fundamental, macro, and single-name/sector research and event-driven analysis** —
demand/supply cycles, earnings previews, pricing forecasts, and positioning into catalysts.

It is deliberately **distinct from the three methodology domains** ([systematic-trading](../systematic-trading/index.md),
[derivatives](../derivatives/index.md), [ml-stats](../ml-stats/index.md)), which are about *how* to
trade. Market-research is about *what is happening in a given market right now* and *what to expect*.
Where a sector's structural traits are cross-cutting, the canonical page lives in
[`shared/instruments`](../shared/index.md) (e.g. [Semiconductors](../shared/instruments/semiconductors.md))
and market-research pages link to it.

## Conventions
- Subfolders on demand: conventionally `sectors/`, `companies/`, `events/`, `macro/`.
- Event/earnings pages are **time-stamped and time-sensitive** — they carry an "as of" date and are
  expected to go stale. Note the as-of date prominently.
- Evidence-based and cited; framed as research, **not investment advice**.
