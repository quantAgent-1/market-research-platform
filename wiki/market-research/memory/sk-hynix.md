## Market reality — the bull case is largely *fact*
- **HBM leadership, durable so far:** **62%** shipment share (Q2'25), **57%** revenue (Q3'25), **58%**
  (Q1'26) per Counterpoint; Goldman sees **>50% through 2026**; **world's-first HBM4 mass-production
  system (Sept 2025)**; UBS sees **~70% of NVIDIA Rubin HBM4**.
- **Record financials (actuals, not forecast):**
  - FY2025: revenue **₩97.2T (+47%)**, op profit **₩47.2T (+101%, 49% margin)**, net income ₩42.9T (+117%).
  - Q4'25: op profit ₩19.2T at a **58% margin**.
  - **Q1'26: op profit ₩37.6T (+405% YoY, ~72% margin).** HBM revenue >2× YoY.

## The valuation crux
**Forward P/E ~7.6 vs trailing ~26.** That *looks* cheap — but the low forward multiple is **entirely
predicated on consensus earnings nearly tripling.** So the entire bull/bear case reduces to one question:

- **Bull:** 2026–27 supercycle earnings are *sustainable* → 7.6× forward is cheap → re-rating room.
- **Bear:** 2025–26 is *peak* earnings → you're paying ~26× trailing on a cyclical top.

That is exactly the [memory: cyclical vs "new era" verdict](cyclical-vs-new-era.md) — for SK Hynix
it's not academic, it *is* the valuation. (That verdict — *dampened, not abolished*; HBM margins now
running *below* commodity DRAM on annual-contract pricing — reframes this: the durable earnings stream is more
HBM-contract-driven than a commodity spike that historically mean-reverts.)

**Now measured** (June 2026 re-rate run `wf_367bbf42-acf`;
[brief](../../sources/deep-research-memory-rerate-2026.md)). The crux above is no longer just framing:
- SK Hynix's year-end **P/B was a flat 0.81-1.87 band across 2017-2024** (no secular drift); the 2025-26
  break to **~4-4.5×** is **one cycle old and arrived at the peak** — the multiple expansion is real but is
  exactly the "peak-cycle expansion" the bear case predicts. [3-0]
- A true **secular premium has not been awarded:** ~6.1× forward (late May 2026) sits far below the Korean
  semi industry (~23×); **even Morgan Stanley still values SK Hynix on a cyclical frame** ("5.3× expected
  2026 earnings / ~4× peak"), and its own sell-side (**Hana Securities**, May 13) concedes the stock still
  "will need… a re-rating of its valuation multiple" — i.e. it *hasn't happened yet*. [3-0]
- The clearest re-categorization *tell* is at Micron, not SK Hynix (Aletheia switching to a growth P/E,
  Jun 15) — and it's **bull-led + a late-cycle "analyst capitulation" pattern.** Meanwhile **foreign
  ownership of Samsung + SK Hynix hit a yearly low** — the opposite of generalist rotation-in.

So, on the crux: **multiple expansion yes, secular re-rate not yet — the market is withholding the premium**
(general mechanics: [fundamental valuation & re-rating](../../shared/concepts/fundamental-valuation.md)).

## Bear case — one verified risk, and an honest gap
- **Verified — HBM4 competition is broadening.** On **June 5, 2026, NVIDIA's Jensen Huang confirmed all
  three (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron) are qualified for Vera Rubin HBM4.** Samsung began HBM4 shipments
  **Feb 2026** (~25-30% share); Korea Herald (Dec'25) said Samsung's HBM4 "delivered the strongest
  overall results" in NVIDIA testing — the gap is narrowing even as SK Hynix still leads ~60-70%. The
  near-monopoly is eroding.
- **Honest gap (not asserted):** CXMT competition, a 2027 commodity-DRAM glut, NVIDIA customer
  concentration, capex intensity, valuation stretch, and Korea-specific (won/governance/tax) risks were
  named but **did not survive verification** this run — flagged open, to be picked up by the cyclicality
  research.
