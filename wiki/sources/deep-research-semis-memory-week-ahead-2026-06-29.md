## Bottom line (the adjudication)
Last week's two-sided chop **despite Micron's beat-and-raise** was **primarily (a) quarter-end / half-year-end
rebalancing + (d) positioning/profit-taking, on a (c) hawkish-macro backdrop — NOT (b) an Apple-triggered
inflation-risk de-risking.** The user's binary resolves as: *"institutional rebalancing"* ✅ largely yes (fused
with positioning); *"inflation-risk de-risking triggered by Apple"* ◐ the **inflation** part is real but runs
through the **hot PCE / hawkish Fed (c)**, **not** through Apple — and the **Apple hike is a *symptom* of the
memory super-cycle (it blames memory-chip cost inflation), which is *bullish* for the memory makers**, not a
demand-destruction signal. This **confirms and sharpens** the wiki's prior read
(Jun-26 note;
[drawdown-flow brief](deep-research-memory-drawdown-flow-2026.md)).

## Verified claim ledger (by driver; vote = adversarial refute-attempts survived)

### (a) Quarter-end / half-year rebalancing — REAL, quantified, a *selling* headwind
- **JPMorgan estimated ~$165B of mechanical equity *selling* into the June 30 quarter/half-year close** (3-0).
  Equities ripped in Q2 → overweight books mechanically sell stocks / buy bonds. [Yahoo/JPM]
- **Russell reconstitution** close is **one of the highest-volume sessions of the year — $217.2B at the June 2025
  close** (3-0) [LSEG] — the scale of late-June mechanical index flow. *(Caveats: the specific "effective Jun 26
  2026" timing was REFUTED 0-3, and "MU reclassified Value→Growth" REFUTED 1-2 — do not assert either.)*
- **GS Prime: semiconductors the most net-sold US subsector** over the prior month (3-0) [GS] — *Caveat: the note
  is dated ~May 22, so it evidences a pre-existing de-risking trend going INTO late June, not a real-time readout.*

### (d) Positioning / profit-taking — the single best-evidenced driver (the Jun-23 Korea leg)
- **Jun 23: KOSPI −9.99% to 8,203.84** (from a record 9,114.55), **circuit breaker** tripped after >8% (3-0);
  **Samsung −12.31% / SK Hynix −12.47%** (3-0), their **worst day since 2008** (2-0). [KoreaTimes / etf.com]
- **Foreign investors net-sold ₩5.79T (~$3.8B)** that session (3-0 / 2-0). [KoreaTimes]
- **Korean single-stock leveraged ETFs = ~14% of Samsung/SK Hynix turnover on Jun 23** (3-0) [Bloomberg/BI via etf.com];
  **16 products launched May 27, $3B day-one → >$9B AUM by Jun 23, ~125%/day turnover** (3-0) [etf.com]; a
  **market-structure quirk** (single-stock futures close 3:45 vs stocks 3:30 → an SK Hynix ETF NAV struck **6-7%
  below** last price on Jun 8) (3-0). Nadig's thesis: the moves were **market-structure + leverage, not
  fundamentals** (3-0). [etf.com]
- Korea Times: the slump stemmed **"largely from profit-taking in semiconductor stocks rather than a broad
  deterioration in macroeconomic conditions"** (3-0). *(Caveat: the Bloomberg "~$6B forced ETF selling" headline
  figure was REFUTED 1-2 — use the 14%-of-turnover figure instead.)*

### (c) Broad macro / Fed — the real "inflation" channel (the Thu/Fri backdrop)
- **Fed (Jun 17, Warsh's first meeting) held 3.50–3.75%; dots 9 hike / 8 hold / 1 cut; 2026 median rate 3.8%
  (from 3.4%)** (3-0). [PIMCO]
- **Fed raised its 2026 PCE forecast to 3.6% (from 2.7%) — attributing it to *energy + the AI build-out*, NOT
  Apple** (3-0). [PIMCO] — the cleanest (c)-over-(b) evidence.
- **May Core PCE = 3.4% YoY** (from 3.3%; vs 3.3% consensus — a slight upside miss), **released Jun 25**;
  **headline PCE 4.1%, a ~3-year high** [BEA/CNBC/CBS]. *(Maintainer-verified; the run only abstained here on the quota.)*

### (b) Apple inflation channel — the WEAKEST leg as a causal de-risking *trigger*
- **Apple (Jun 25) hiked Mac/iPad prices up to ~$300** (Mac Studio M3 Ultra **+$1,300** to $5,299; MacBook Air
  $1,099→$1,299; iPad Pro $999→$1,199); **iPhone/Watch/AirPods unchanged**; **explicitly blamed memory/storage
  chip-cost inflation from AI data-center demand** ("we have never seen a component price increase this much, this
  quickly"); **Microsoft hiked too**. **AAPL closed −6% (worst since Apr 2025), ~$265B wiped.** [CBS/CNBC/MacRumors/9to5/NBC]
  *(Maintainer-verified facts; the run abstained on the causal link.)*
- CNBC (Jun 26) attributed the Friday chip selloff to **"rising AI-infrastructure costs" / "who is on the hook for
  memory bills"** [CNBC] — i.e. a *margin/cost-allocation* anxiety hitting device makers & hyperscalers, **not a
  memory-demand-destruction signal**. *(Extracted, not adversarially verified — quota.)*
- **Why (b) is weak:** (i) the biggest move (Jun 23) **pre-dates** the Apple hike (Jun 25); (ii) Apple blamed
  **memory-cost inflation = the super-cycle**, which is **bullish for MU/Samsung/SK Hynix** (they get the pricing);
  (iii) the institutional inflation case (the Fed's own forecast) rests on **energy + AI capex**, not Apple; (iv)
  no evidence the hike moved breakevens or the Fed path specifically.

### The four requested institutional notes
1. **SemiAnalysis — "China's CXMT…"** ✅ ~70-75% readable (primary, fetched). CXMT = **#4 DRAM maker**, ~**265→350
   (2026)→420 (2027, ~17% of capacity)→500 kwspm (2028)**; **bit share ~9%→12% by 2027 (~3% gain)** in a ~$1T market.
   1Q26 **ASP only 5-10% below incumbents but cost/bit >30% higher → riding the cycle, NOT dumping**; HBM ~1% of rev.
   **DRAM stays undersupplied through ~2028** (high-single-digit % 2026 → low-mid-teens 2027), **prices double again
   in 2026**, and SemiAnalysis judges **CXMT fears "overplayed at least for the next two years"** (multiple 3-0).
   → **The China-glut bear leg is a 2028+ tail, not a near-term driver** — i.e. last week was *not* a fundamental crack.
2. **Goldman Sachs — "CPU Server Demand… Agentic AI"** ❌ client-only; strong secondary. Agentic AI shifts the
   **CPU:GPU ratio from ~1:4-1:8 toward 1:1-1:2**; enterprise server rev **+20% (2026) / +26% (2027)** → a **DRAM
   content tailwind** (the note behind GS's AMD upgrade). [Investing/Futu]
3. **Morgan Stanley — "Old Memory: Better to Buy More"** ❌ client-only; strong secondary. Bullish legacy DRAM: 8Gb
   DDR4 contract **+752%** (Feb-25 $1.35 → Feb-26 $11.50), Q1-26 contract **+100%**, **2026 DRAM +62% / NAND +75%**,
   "the cycle has only just begun." [Tom's Hardware / Investing / iTiger]
4. **J.P. Morgan — "First Principles — Following the Power"** ⚠️ specific equity note not public; only JPM's public
   "follow the power" theme (power = the AI bottleneck; "speed to power"; ~+100 GW by 2030 — the capex-sustainability
   leg). [JPM]
   → **3 of 4 notes are bullish/structural-demand; the 4th (CXMT supply) reads "not yet a threat"** — reinforcing
   that the fundamentals are strong and the volatility was flows/macro.
