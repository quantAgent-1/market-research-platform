# Euan Sinclair

Option trader (b. 1969) and author known for pragmatic, quantitative volatility trading. Writes as
a practitioner — "my success is measured in profits" — favouring simple, robust tools over elaborate
models.

## Key works
- *[Volatility Trading](../../sources/sinclair-volatility-trading.md)* (Wiley, 2008) — measurement,
  forecasting, hedging, and [Kelly-based money management](../concepts/kelly-criterion.md).
- *Option Trading* (Wiley, 2010) — a complementary practitioner volume (not yet ingested).

## Ideas associated with him (in this wiki)
- [Volatility arbitrage](../../derivatives/strategies/volatility-arbitrage.md): trade forecast RV
  vs. market IV.
- Heavy emphasis on **position sizing** ([Kelly](../concepts/kelly-criterion.md)) and disciplined
  trade evaluation.
