# Conviction-Swing on Institutional Accumulation

A discretionary multi-week to multi-month swing **template** for high-conviction, catalyst-rich names. It pairs an entry edge (reading accumulation) with the discipline that edge often lacks: **holding to a forward target**.

Built on [swing trading](swing-trading.md) and [momentum](../factors-signals/momentum.md). **Education, not investment advice.**

## Diagnosis — the pattern this fixes

A recurring operator failure (ticker-independent):

1. **Edge:** a correct accumulation read near a base.
2. **Leak:** selling the first consolidation (“feels like a trap”) — capturing a fraction of the full move.
3. **Compounding error:** re-buying higher after watching it run (worse risk/reward).
4. **Root cause:** no pre-written forward target + timeframe mismatch (judging a multi-week thesis on minute bars).

This is the reverse [disposition effect](../../shared/concepts/disposition-effect.md): cutting winners from discomfort.

## The system

### 1. Entry — accumulation read

Enter on persistent bid / dips bought / volume on strength near a base. See [informed vs uninformed flow](../../shared/concepts/informed-vs-uninformed-flow.md) for what the read can and cannot claim.

### 2. Forward target before size

Write thesis + target *before* sizing:

> Accumulating near X because forward earnings path → E and multiple framework → region T.

Targets come from research ([fundamental valuation](../../shared/concepts/fundamental-valuation.md), sector supply-demand notes), not feel.

### 3. Hold — chop vs distribution

- **Continuation:** higher lows, dips bought, volume dries on pullbacks → hold.
- **Distribution:** lower highs, dips not bought, rallies sold → exit process.

Judge multi-week holds on **daily/weekly** structure. Top-side tells: [distribution & pullback tells](../../shared/concepts/distribution-and-pullback-tells.md).

### 4. Exit — objective tripwires

Exit on first of: (a) target reached with froth/distribution; (b) thesis invalidation (research tripwires — e.g. forward revision inflection, demand/supply regime break); (c) structural price invalidation (break of consolidation floor / key MA chosen in advance). See [stop-losses & exits](../../shared/concepts/stop-losses-and-exits.md) and [tripwire exit machine](../checklists/tripwire-exit-execution.md).

### 5. No-chase

Do not re-enter at a worse price unless the thesis **re-justifies** at the new level (fresh base or raised research target), not FOMO.

### 6. Sizing & survival

Concentration only when the thesis and invalidation are written; always subject to [risk of ruin](../../shared/concepts/risk-of-ruin.md) and the [risk constitution](../checklists/trading-risk-constitution.md). Prefer reducing **leverage and redundancy** over panic-stopping a cash core inside normal noise.

## What this is not

- Not a prediction that any name reaches a target.
- Not a substitute for cost/tax awareness or a validated statistical edge ([retail-capital edge map](../../synthesis/retail-capital-edge-map.md)).
- Not a license to ignore forced-flow regimes ([multi-week unwinds](../../shared/concepts/multi-week-unwinds-and-recoveries.md)).
