# The Memory Supercycle & the Micron Earnings Catalyst — Deep-Research Brief

**Source:** Claude Code `deep-research` run `wf_f4f5baf1-5a2` (2026-06-20).
**Method:** 5 angles → 23 sources → 98 claims → 25 adversarially verified → **19 confirmed, 6 refuted →
12 findings.** The hard *facts* rest on primary releases (TrendForce, Broadcom 8-K, Micron's IR date
notice); the *forecasts* (2Q26/2027 prices, the BofA HBM-market size, the wafer mix) are labelled as
such. Several share/consensus figures lean on secondary aggregators (Zacks/AlphaStreet, Omdia via
press, Counterpoint via relay) — flagged inline.
**Purpose:** answer four questions as of **2026-06-20**, four days before Micron's print: (1) the state
of memory demand, (2) the Micron FQ3-2026 earnings setup, (3) whether MU faces "another AVGO selloff,"
and (4) long-term memory pricing to end-2027. **Evidence/education, not investment advice.**

## The one-paragraph answer
The memory complex is in a **strong, HBM-led up-cycle** that TrendForce and BofA call a **"supercycle."**
2Q26 contract prices are forecast up **58–63% QoQ (DRAM)** and **70–75% QoQ (NAND)** — steep but
*decelerating* from 1Q26's record ~90–95%. **HBM is sold out for 2026** and is structurally **crowding
out commodity DRAM** (HBM = ~18/22/30% of the top-three's DRAM *wafer* input by end-2025/26/27, but only
~8/9/13% of *bit* supply — it eats 3–4× the area per GB). **Micron reports FQ3-2026 after the close on
Wed June 24, 2026**, with consensus (~**$19.72 EPS / $34.52B rev**) sitting *above* its own raised
guidance and the stock at an **all-time high ~$1,134 (+756% in a year)** — a setup that makes
**guidance/sustainability commentary more decisive than the headline beat** and elevates sell-the-news
risk. The direct precedent: **Broadcom beat across the board (AI rev +143% to $10.8B) yet fell ~14%**
because it didn't raise its long-term $100B AI target. On the long arc, analysts expect shortages to
**ease by 2027–2028** while **pricing stays elevated through end-2027**; **HBM is expected to stay
tight** while the principal tail risk — a **commodity-DRAM glut** — concentrates in conventional DRAM
(Big-Three + China capacity).

## Confirmed findings

### Q1 — State of memory demand (mid-2026)
- **AI/HBM-led up-cycle; steep but decelerating commodity prices.** 2Q26 conventional DRAM contract
  **+58–63% QoQ**, NAND **+70–75% QoQ** (TrendForce 20260331, forecast) — *down* from 1Q26's record
  ~90–95%. Driver is AI-server / capacity-reallocation; PC DRAM is relatively soft. [3-0]
- **CSPs locking supply.** North-American CSPs accelerating AI **inference** deployments and signing
  **3–5yr LTAs** at higher prices → supplier pricing power; ~20–30% of DDR bits projected LTA-locked
  by 2027. [3-0]
- **Disciplined oligopoly + HBM sold out.** Big Three ≈ **90% of DRAM** revenue (Q1'26: Samsung 38.6%
  / SK Hynix 28.8% / Micron 22.4% = 89.8%); **HBM sold out for 2026** (*primary*: SK Hynix CFO + Micron).
  Caveat: China's **CXMT is now #4 (~7.5%)**, so on a *capacity* (not revenue) basis the "90–95%" top is
  generous. [2-1]
- **"Supercycle."** BofA: **2026 HBM market $54.6B (+58% YoY)**, "supercycle similar to the 1990s boom"
  (recurs across outlets; SK Hynix newsroom is vendor-interested). [3-0]
- **HBM crowds out commodity DRAM.** HBM = **~18/22/30%** of top-three DRAM **wafer** input by
  end-2025/26/27, but only **~8/9/13%** of **bit** supply — HBM consumes ~3–4× wafer area per GB
  (TrendForce 20260602). SK Hynix individually ~30%→~40%. [3-0]
- **HBM share is contested/volatile** (historical context): Q2'25 was SK Hynix 62% / Micron 21% /
  Samsung 17% — but it **flipped back in Q3'25** (Samsung 35% / Micron 11% / SK Hynix 53% — ⚠️ *corrected 2026-06-21: independent verification gives Q3'25 as SK Hynix ~57% / Samsung ~22% / Micron ~21% per Counterpoint; use those*). Micron's #2
  HBM slot was **not durable**; present only as a dated snapshot. [3-0]

### Q2 — Micron FQ3-2026 preview
- **Date (FACT):** after close **Wed June 24, 2026, 2:30pm MT** — fiscal **Q3 FY2026** (Q2 was reported
  Mar 18, 2026). [3-0]
- **Consensus:** ~**$19.72 EPS** on ~**$34.52B revenue** (Zacks/AlphaStreet, 31 analysts; EPS range
  $7.53–$24.08 — wide dispersion). EPS revised **+3.1% (30d) / +68.1% (90d)** — but the 90-day jump is
  largely a **mechanical catch-up** to Micron's own raised guide ($19.15 / $33.5B), not pure new
  bullishness. The Street sits **above** guidance → **raised bar**. Aggregators vary ($19.63–$20.25 EPS;
  $34.43–$35.02B rev). [2-1]
- **Run-up.** Stock at an **all-time high ~$1,134** (Jun 18 close; ~$1,150+ Jun 19), **+~756% in a year**
  — substantial AI optimism priced in; "guidance and sustainability commentary as important as the
  beat" (AlphaStreet). BofA's $950 target (May 13) was *already left behind* within ~5 weeks. [3-0]

### Q3 — Post-earnings reaction & the AVGO precedent
- **AVGO beat then fell.** Broadcom FQ2-2026 (qtr ended May 3): AI semi revenue **+143% YoY to $10.8B**,
  total **$22.2B (+48%)**, adj EPS **$2.44 (+54%, beat $2.40)** — revenue beat the sell-side average but
  narrowly missed the strictest LSEG consensus ($22.19B vs $22.27B). [3-0]
- **~14% after-hours drop (Jun 3)** despite the beat — a textbook **sell-the-news** — after running
  **~40% YTD (vs Nasdaq 16%), ~9× since end-2022.** Trigger: Hock Tan **did not raise the long-term
  $100B AI target**, and the near-term AI guide (~$16B vs ~$17.2B hoped) disappointed. *Caveat:* total
  Q3 revenue guidance (~$29.4B) actually **exceeded** consensus (~$28.53B) — it was the *long-term
  target held flat* + soft near-term AI guide, not an overall guidance cut. **Directly analogous setup
  to MU.** [3-0]

### Q4 — Long-term pricing to end-2027
- **HBM "surge multiples higher" in 2027** (TrendForce 20260602) on the HBM4 negotiation cycle +
  crowding-out (HBM ~30% of top-three wafer starts by 2027). **The multiple is NOT quantified by
  TrendForce** — the "double" figure in secondary coverage is editorial. [3-0]
- **Shortages ease by 2027–2028, but prices stay elevated through end-2027** on sustained AI demand +
  rising capital intensity (Gartner: "high through to end of 2027"); some see the rally past 2028
  (Samsung/SK Hynix cautious on expansion). **Bull = sustained HBM tightness; bear = commodity-DRAM
  glut** from Big-Three + China (CXMT/YMTC) capacity. **Critical split: any 2027 oversupply hits
  commodity DRAM — HBM is expected to stay tight.** [3-0, medium confidence: forecast]

## Caveats
- **Forecast vs fact:** only the June-24 date, AVGO's results + ~14% drop, MU's ~$1,134 run-up, and
  Q1/Q2-2026 realized shares are *facts*. All 2Q26/2027 prices, the $54.6B HBM market, the 18/22/30%
  mix, and the easing timeline are **forecasts**.
- **Source tiers:** TrendForce + Broadcom 8-K + Micron IR notice are primary; MU consensus (Zacks/
  AlphaStreet), the BofA figures (SK Hynix newsroom), and HBM shares (Counterpoint via relay) are
  secondary/multi-hop.
- **Time-sensitive:** a snapshot 4 days pre-print; prices, the implied move, and consensus will move
  into and through June 24.
