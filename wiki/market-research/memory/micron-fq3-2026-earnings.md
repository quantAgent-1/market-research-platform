## The AVGO precedent — a beat that still sold off
Broadcom FQ2-2026 (qtr ended May 3, 2026) is the live template the question invokes:
- **Beat across the board:** AI semiconductor revenue **+143% YoY to $10.8B**, total revenue **$22.2B
  (+48%)**, adj EPS **$2.44 (+54%, beat $2.40)**. (Revenue beat the sell-side average; narrowly missed
  the *strictest* LSEG consensus $22.19B vs $22.27B.)
- **Fell ~14% after-hours (Jun 3)** anyway — after running **~40% YTD / ~9× since end-2022.**
- **Why:** Hock Tan **did not raise** the long-term **$100B AI target**, and the near-term AI guide
  (~$16B vs ~$17.2B hoped) disappointed. *Nuance:* total Q3 revenue guidance (~$29.4B) actually
  **exceeded** consensus (~$28.53B) — so it was the *long-term target held flat* + a soft near-term AI
  line, **not** an overall guidance cut. Records weren't enough; the *forward narrative* was.

## Will MU be "another AVGO selloff"?
**The setup is strikingly analogous → beat-and-selloff risk is genuinely elevated, but not destiny.**

- **Why the risk is real:** MU walks in at an all-time high, +756%/yr, with consensus *above* guidance.
  A *mere beat is largely priced in*; the reaction will hinge on **guidance + sustainability commentary**,
  exactly the axis on which AVGO disappointed. The stretched chart makes profit-taking the path of least
  resistance on anything short of a raise.
- **Why it could break the other way:** AVGO's sin was a *failure to raise* its long-term target. Micron
  has a credible path to **do what AVGO didn't** — HBM is **sold out through 2026** and TrendForce
  expects HBM to **"surge multiples higher" in 2027.** A convincingly raised HBM/2027 outlook can carry
  the stock through the run. Memory is also more cyclical/cheaper on earnings than AVGO's custom-silicon
  franchise — different valuation base.
- **Asymmetry:** high bar, large downside on any blemish, limited upside on an in-line beat.

### Scenarios (qualitative — no verified probabilities) — ⛳ resolved Jun 24
| Scenario | What drives it | Likely reaction | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beat-and-rally** | Blowout **plus** raised HBM/2027 outlook or new targets | Can rally through the run — the AVGO escape hatch | ✅ **This happened** — blowout + sold-out-CY2027 raise → +15.7% new ATH close |

| **Miss** | HBM ramp / gross-margin / ASP disappointment | Outsized drop given valuation; unlikely given the raised guide | ❌ Did not occur |

## ✅ Verified & re-derived from first principles (2026-06-22 — 2 days pre-print)
A third deep-research run ([swing & position brief](../../sources/deep-research-micron-swing-position-2026.md),
`wf_a7f8b589-e70`; 23 sources → 25 verified → **15 confirmed / 10 killed**; walled on the quota at the verify
phase, **resumed past the 20:50 KST reset**) re-derived the swing math, **corrected several earlier figures**,
and built a hold-vs-sell framework. Corrections in **bold**.

**Swing magnitude — corrected.** At IV **~103%** (IV-rank ~95), a *one-day* implied move is only **~5.4%**
(S·IV·√(1/365)) — so the headline "~14–17%" figures are a **WEEKLY straddle** (to Fri Jun 26), **not one-day**.
Best-sourced weekly ≈ **14.3%**; decomposing it implies a **~8–9% discrete earnings event-jump**. **The "~17%"
(refuted 1-2) and "~11%" (refuted 0-3) one-day figures are wrong — use ~14% weekly / ~8–9% event-jump.** Realized
history (robust): **~6.53% avg over 20 quarters**, **~4.4% last 4Q**; **fat tails** (the last 8 prints include a
+23.8% and a −13.8%). The implied-vs-realized gap is a **~2× volatility-risk premium on average** — but **NOT a
per-print guarantee** (realized *exceeded* implied in 4 of the last 8 quarters).
→ **In dollars on ~$1,134:** typical realized ±~$74; priced *event* ±~$95–100; full-week straddle (incl. PCE)
±~$160; historical *tail* −$157 to +$270.

**Direction — refined.** The AVGO precedent holds but was **not a "clean beat across the board"**: AVGO beat
adj-EPS but **narrowly *missed* revenue** ($22.19B vs $22.27B), and fell **~12–15%** on its *failure to raise*
the $100B AI target. For MU, the bullish **"raise"** — a numerical HBM-share target, a raised/pulled-forward
$100B HBM TAM, or a **sold-out-2027** commitment — is **precisely what management has historically *withheld***
(only CY2026 is confirmed sold out), which tilts the asymmetry toward the AVGO outcome. *(⛳ Resolved Jun 24:
management broke the pattern and **delivered the withheld raise** — the tilt read was reasonable ex-ante but the
**beat-and-rally** branch is what happened; see the [post-mortem](#post-mortem-resolved--filled-2026-07-02).)*
Positioning is
directionally crowded-bullish, but the **specific analyst-target list** (avg ~$1,091, Cantor $1,500, etc.) **could
not be verified** (refuted 0-3 as a sourced set) — cite it as directional only.

**The leveraged read-through (SOXL).** SOXL is **3× the *daily*** semiconductor index (MU a top constituent; the
exact ~7% weight is a working assumption — issuer weights refuted). A MU move contributes only **~1.4–3.6%** to
SOXL *directly*; the realistic **±10–15%+** event-window swing is **sympathy + Thursday's macro ×3 + decay**.
Holding a 3×-daily ETF through a multi-day, two-catalyst window is structurally costly — see
[leveraged-ETF decay](../../shared/concepts/leveraged-etf-decay.md).

**Hold-vs-sell framework (education, not advice).** Realized is ~centered with no verified directional edge, so
**IV is the market's fair price of the gap → a stock-holder's event EV ≈ ~0 drift / huge variance** unless one
holds a real edge on the guidance. The asymmetry is **unfavorable for a stretched ATH name** (large downside on
any blemish/no-raise; limited upside on an in-line beat); **only a convincing forward RAISE flips it.** A
concentrated MU + leveraged-semis book is the *same* bet doubled — the leveraged leg adds leverage + decay + 3×
macro. **Flips toward holding:** an explicit raise. **Confirms caution:** in-line guidance, no raise, a
2027-softness hint, or a hot PCE. *(The macro overlay was not re-verified this run — see the
[week-ahead](../weekly/week-ahead-2026-06-22.md).)*

## The pre-print DRAWDOWN — de-risking, not informed flow (as of 2026-06-24)
A fourth deep-research run ([drawdown / flow-forensics brief](../../sources/deep-research-memory-drawdown-flow-2026.md),
`wf_bde6e4ab-3da`; 25 sources → 101 claims → 25 verified → **23 confirmed / 2 killed**; clean one pass)
adversarially tested the question **"is the pre-print memory-stock drawdown benign de-risking, or informed/
insider flow about the print?"** — with **de-risking + macro set as the NULL** and informed flow required to
clear a high bar. **Verdict: the NULL wins decisively.**

**What happened (FACT).** MU **fell −13.18% on Tue Jun 23** — the session before the print — from an **ATH close
of $1,211.38 (Jun 22)** to **$1,051.77** (worst day since Jun 5). It was **not** the calm tape (VIX ~16.8) this
page assumed: Jun 23 was a **circuit-breaker panic** (KOSPI −9.99%, halted *twice*).

**Why it's de-risking, not informed flow — tell by tell:**
- **Breadth (the decisive tell):** the *whole complex* fell in lockstep — Samsung ~−12%, SK Hynix ~−12.5%,
  Kioxia ~−15%, SanDisk ~−11%, WDC ~−10%; SOX −7.87%, SMH −7.01%. **MU's drop was IN LINE with its true peers,
  not beyond them.** It was the biggest *US* decliner (~13% vs NVDA/AMD/Intel −3% to −5%) only because it's the
  purest US proxy for the Korean memory names, the most stretched (+756%), and faced a binary print next day —
  the **highest beta** to a memory-led risk-off. The idiosyncratic MU-only weakness an informed-flow read
  *requires* is **absent.**
- **Exogenous, non-Micron trigger:** a Korea-centered spark — the **MSCI developed-market rejection**
  (primary-confirmed, ~Jun 23) + a **leveraged single-stock ETF / margin unwind** (the FSS *regretted approving*
  them) + extreme **concentration/valuation** (Samsung+Hynix ≈ 48% of the KOSPI; SK Hynix +190% YTD) — on a
  crowded *global* AI/memory trade. Korea crashed in the **Asian session before the US opened** (KOSPI −9.99%,
  ~**$3.8B foreign net selling**), and US memory/semis sympathized on the open + a same-day hawkish BofA rate
  note. **The regulator named no US chipmaker; the same day BofA *raised* its MU target to $1,500** — the opposite
  of a "print is bad" tell. *(NB: "Korea-domestic" vs. "US-institutions de-grossing" is a false dichotomy — the
  big Korean sellers were foreign/global funds. See the [brief's follow-up verification](../../sources/deep-research-memory-drawdown-flow-2026.md).)*
- **Literal insider trading is the least likely channel:** insiders are in a pre-earnings **blackout**; Rule
  10b-5 + 10b5-1 cooling-off rules block freshly-timed sales; insider activity clusters *after* prints and
  accompanies only ~5% of announcements. (Broad "informed flow" — channel checks / pricing reads — isn't ruled
  out, but it's diffuse and partly already in consensus/IV, not a smoking gun.)
- **Base rate:** a pre-earnings drawdown **does not predict the print.** Pre-earnings drift in stretched
  high-flyers is mostly uninformed over-extrapolation that **mean-reverts** — it forecasts *reversion*, not the
  surprise direction. "Smart money is selling, so the print is bad" is **not** a reliable inference. The real
  asymmetry comes from the **earnings torpedo** (glamour names punished disproportionately *on a miss*) — a
  statement about the reaction *conditional* on a miss, not its odds. See
  [informed vs. uninformed flow](../../shared/concepts/informed-vs-uninformed-flow.md).

**For the research book (education, not advice):** at ~$1,052, **MU @ $1,115 is ≈ −5.6% underwater**, and the
**SOXL leg took the worse hit** (SOX −7.87% × 3×-daily ≈ −23% that session; exact mark not pulled). The drop
signals ~nothing about tonight's number — the genuine risk is being **stretched, concentrated, and leveraged
into a binary with an asymmetric reaction function**, not that the drawdown is a "warning."

## Post-mortem (resolved — filled 2026-07-02)

Filled from the verified record in the Jun-26 post-mortem note;
graded against the preview above.

**The print (Wed Jun 24 AMC) — blowout beat AND the withheld raise:**
- **Adj EPS $25.11** vs the ~$19.72 pre-print bar (~$20.49 final at-print consensus → **+22.6% beat**);
  **revenue $41.46B** vs ~$34.52B / ~$35.69B (**+16.2%**). Gross margin ~**80%+ actual, guided ~81%**.
- **The raise — exactly what the preview said management had "historically withheld":** **HBM3E & HBM4
  sold out through calendar 2027, demand into 2028** ("far above supply"); **FY26 capex →~$27B (+$5B),
  FY27 → mid-$40B+**; **16 LTAs / $22B commitments / $18B cash deposits**; RPO ~$100B (one outlet).

**The reaction (verified OHLC):** AH **+14.55% to $1,199.52**; Thu 6/25 opened **$1,233** (above the AH
print), spiked to **$1,255**, faded ~9% intraday to $1,136, closed **$1,213.56 (+15.7%)** — a **new ATH
close, but only +0.18% above the Jun-22 peak**. The repricing happened AH/overnight; the cash session gave
back an overnight gain rather than building one.

**Scenario grade: Beat-and-rally (the AVGO escape hatch) — the branch this page rated possible-but-not-base-case.**
The preview's directional lean ("tilts the asymmetry toward the AVGO outcome") was **wrong at the print**:
management broke its withholding pattern and delivered the sold-out-2027 commitment. The lean was reasonable
ex-ante (it was the documented pattern), and the page's own escape-hatch condition — "a convincing forward
RAISE flips it" — is precisely what triggered.

**Swing-magnitude grade:** realized **+15.7% close-to-close (+17% gap)** vs the **~8–9% priced event-jump**
and the **~6.5% historical average** — realized *exceeded* implied this time. Consistent with the brief's own
caveat: the ~2× volatility-risk premium is an **average, not a per-print guarantee** (realized had exceeded
implied in 4 of the prior 8 quarters); this print was one of the fat tails.

**The aftermath (the part no scenario table covered):** within 3 sessions the pop **fully round-tripped on
flows** — Fri 6/26 −6.69% to $1,132.33; Mon 6/29 undercut the Jun-23 panic low intraday (**$1,023.65,
−9.6%**) then **V-reversed to close green $1,145.28 (+1.14%)**. Diagnosed as **quarter-end/mechanical
positioning, not a fundamental crack** — see the [positioning pages](../positioning/unwind-exhausted-or-just-starting-2026-06-29.md)
and the [unwind-duration note](../positioning/unwind-duration-and-flows-2026-06-30.md). Net lesson: the
event edge (beat-and-raise) and the flow regime (forced de-risking) were **two separate games run on the
same tape in the same week** — being right on the print did not exempt the position from the flows.
