# How Long the Memory/Semis Unwind Lasts — Live Flows & Duration (June 30, 2026)

> **As of 2026-06-30 (Tue, quarter/half-year-end close). Ephemeral — resolves over ~2 weeks.**
> Education, **not advice.** Full verified ledger + provenance in the
> [brief](../../sources/deep-research-semis-memory-unwind-timeframe-2026-06-30.md).
> **Confidence: MEDIUM** — per-flow cadences are grounded; the *summed* duration is an estimate, not a
> measurement, and nearly all exposure **levels** are stale (the durable input is **cadence**, not magnitude).

## The answer
The post-Micron sell-off is a **flows-driven unwind**, and live positioning bounds its **acute mechanical
phase at ~2 weeks — with the bulk of the calendar-rebalancing pressure clearing at/around the June 30
quarter-end close, not persisting as a July overhang.** Behind it sits a **slower hedge-fund de-gross /
vol-control re-leverage tail of a further ~1–2 weeks into mid-July.** The **dominant swing variable is the
July 2 US jobs print** — a hot read can push price through the next CTA trigger (~3% lower S&P / ~5% NDX)
and **re-accelerate the systematic leg**.

**The spine of the call** is a genuinely *current* anchor the wiki didn't have: **Citadel Securities'
Scott Rubner (Jun 17) flagged the "next two weeks" (~Jun 17–Jul 1) as "driven more by market mechanics
than fundamentals"** — the Jun 18 record $8.3T OPEX, the Russell recon, the Jun 30 quarter-end — adding
**"the setup looks favorable beyond quarter-end."** The late-June Micron unwind sits squarely inside it.

| **Hedge funds** (discretionary) | **Semis the most net-sold US subsector** heading in; **trimming longs**, not new shorts | **Multi-week** ("weeks," not days) — the **slow tail** | **STALE** (Apr–May); cadence prior only |

**Two amplifiers, not dampeners** (levered ETFs + vol-control) front-load the move; **one slow tail**
(HF de-gross); the **calendar leg clears at quarter-end**. That shape is *why* the acute phase is short
and violent rather than a long grind.

## Expected path & falsifiable tells
- **Bounce / rebalancing-was-dominant:** stabilization *after* the **Jun 30 close** ⇒ the mechanical bulk
  has cleared. (Consistent with the [Jun-29 tactical read's](unwind-exhausted-or-just-starting-2026-06-29.md)
  ~55–60% bounce base case.)
- **Slow tail still running:** selling persists into **Jul 1–3 with no new catalyst** ⇒ HF de-gross /
  vol-control tail not yet done.
- **Second leg fires:** a **hot Jul-2 jobs print** breaches the BofA trigger ⇒ systematic re-acceleration
  (the [Jun-29 page's](unwind-exhausted-or-just-starting-2026-06-29.md) ~40% conditional tail).
- **Tail ending:** realized-vol decay + **VIX re-contango** ⇒ vol-control begins rebuilding (a *buy-side*
  flow returns).

**Calendar:** Jun 26 Russell (done) → **Jun 30 quarter-end close** → Jul 1 new cycle (*not* a clean flip
to buying) → **Jul 2 US jobs (the variable)** → Jul 3 US closed → **mid-July Q2 earnings** (fundamentals reassert).

## Caveats
- **Staleness is the dominant caveat:** exposure **levels** are pre-unwind (Apr–mid-June); the analysis
  rests on **cadence/mechanism**, not verified current magnitude. The **largest single unknown** is how
  much CTA/HF dry powder was **already discharged in the Jun 23 flush** vs. remains.
- **Refuted — do NOT use:** clean Jul-1 flip to buying; dealers long-gamma in semis; a fixed ~1-month
  vol-control re-leverage clock; ">$100B CTA selling"; Jun-23 VIX backwardation; "forced MU Russell
  rebalance caused −6.7%." (Votes in the [brief](../../sources/deep-research-semis-memory-unwind-timeframe-2026-06-30.md#caveats--limitations--staleness-is-the-dominant-caveat).)
- **Biggest gaps:** live Korea foreign flows + regulator action; live semis dealer-gamma direction;
  semis-specific IV-rank/put-call/VIX-term/COT — all unmeasured.
