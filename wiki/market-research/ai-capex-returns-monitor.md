# AI Capex vs. Returns — What to Watch

> **Reference checklist (as of 2026-06-24).** The **framework is evergreen**; the **current readings go
> stale** — refresh the live numbers periodically. This is a working note, **not** a research brief.
> Education, not advice.

**Why this exists.** Whether the AI data-center buildout is a *durable, self-sustaining* investment cycle —
or a buildout heading for an air-pocket — is the **master variable** behind the
[memory/semis thesis](memory/cyclical-vs-new-era.md). The discipline: **follow the money** — spend → how it's
financed → whether it earns a return → memory read-through. It is durable **iff returns eventually justify the
spend**; busts start on the **financing** side or the **returns** side.

## 1. The spend — growing, decelerating, or rolling over?
- **Hyperscaler capex** (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, + ORCL): the $ level, the YoY growth rate, **the second
  derivative** (accelerating vs. just decelerating), and **forward capex guidance**. *The guide > the print.*
- **NVIDIA data-center revenue + guidance** — the cleanest real-time proxy for the whole buildout; Blackwell/
  Rubin ramp, backlog, lead times.
- **Neoclouds** (CoreWeave, Nebius, Crusoe…) + **sovereign AI** — faster-growing, more debt-financed (also a §2 flag).

## 2. The financing — red flags (where busts start)
- **Cash-flow-funded vs. debt-funded.** FCF-funded = durable; a drift toward **debt / SPVs / private-credit /
  off-balance-sheet** data-center financing = late-cycle stress. Watch **capex vs. FCF**.
- **Depreciation life assumptions** — *lengthening* GPU/server useful life flatters earnings now but bets the
  gear lasts (the Burry critique); a yellow flag.
- **Circular financing** — supplier investing in its own customers / vendor financing (demand funded by the seller).

## 3. The returns — the crux (does the spend earn money?)
- **The AI revenue gap** (Sequoia's "$600B question"): cumulative AI **capex** vs. AI **revenue** — need it
  **narrowing, not necessarily closed**.
- **App-layer revenue run-rates** — OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft Copilot/Azure AI, Google Cloud AI.
- **Cloud growth re-acceleration** + the AI-attributed share (Azure/AWS/GCP).
- **Usage / token volume** — inference workload + API-call growth (**agentic workloads multiply tokens/task**);
  **falling cost-per-token *with* sustained pricing power** = the healthy signature.

## 4. Memory read-through — translate to MU / SK Hynix earnings
- **HBM supply/demand balance**; sold-out status; HBM4 ramp. *(HBM TAM guided ~$35B (2025) → ~$100B (2028).)*
- **DRAM/NAND contract ASPs + bit-shipment growth** (TrendForce / DRAMeXchange) — **the price series *is* the
  cycle tell**; spot leads contract.
- **Supplier capex discipline** (the 3 makers over-investing = the classic cycle-killer); wafer-capacity adds.
- **Customer inventory (days of inventory)** — rising = digestion coming. **Semi-equipment book-to-bill**
  (ASML/AMAT/Lam) = forward indicator for the whole chain.

## Watch only five
1. Hyperscaler **forward capex guides** (durability of the spend) ·
2. The **AI-revenue-gap trajectory** (returns catching up — or not) ·
3. **DRAM/HBM contract pricing** (the cycle in real time) ·
4. **Depreciation / financing** red flags (how the spend is funded) ·
5. **Customer inventory / digestion** signals (early warning of a pause).

## The physical-AI upside leg (optionality, not the base case)
Robotics / autonomous systems / AI-run manufacturing push inference compute + memory **out of the data center
to the edge** — a far larger TAM, and the *real* return story: **productivity → lower marginal cost →
deflationary force → more consumer wealth.** If it lands, the AI-revenue gap closes decisively and the capex was
never a bubble. Two tracking caveats (not skepticism):
- It's a **2027–2030+** driver, not 2026–27 — watch **humanoid pilot deployments, factory-automation adoption,
  autonomous-vehicle miles, edge-inference chip shipments**.
- The **productivity J-curve**: general-purpose tech delivers gains lagged and lumpy, so an **air-pocket between
  capex (now) and payoff (later)** is possible *even if the vision is right*. The vision being correct and a
  memory down-cycle in 2027–28 are not mutually exclusive — **timing is the whole game.**
