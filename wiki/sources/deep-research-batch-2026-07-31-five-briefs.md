largest in US history, incl. a $10B Berkshire private placement — verified on Alphabet IR to the
per-share price)** plus $31.5B of multi-currency bonds including a 100-year sterling tranche.
Meta: $55B of bonds since Oct-2025 plus the $27B Hyperion SPV (Blue Owl 80/20, PIMCO $18B anchor,
A+ paper at 6.58%). Amazon: ~$62B of bonds YTD. Oracle: BBB−, CDS ~200–215bp, FCF deeply negative,
$638B RPO with the OpenAI share unverifiable. CoreWeave: ~$25B debt, 5y CDS ~855bp (~50% implied
5y default probability), but also the first investment-grade GPU-backed facility (DDTL 4.0, $8.5B,
Moody's A3, SOFR+225). xAI: a $20B round structured ~$7.5B equity + $12.5B SPV debt that buys
Nvidia chips and rents them back — the circular structure in its purest form. OpenAI: $122B round
closed Mar-31 at $852B post-money. Data-center ABS/CMBS: ~$26–27B issued 2025, ~80% of post-early-
2025 paper trading wider than issue. NVDA–OpenAI $250B/$350B guarantees: **still in talks, not
signed** — correctly labeled, fastest-moving fact in the file. Five falsifiable T2 tripwires with
named sources (Oracle loses IG · CRWV CDS >1,000bp sustained/failed refi · guarantee

**Corrections:** Nebius 2026 capex guide is stale ($16–20B in the report; raised to ~$25B at the
