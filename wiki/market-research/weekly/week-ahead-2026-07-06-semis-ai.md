## The analysis: a crowding purge inside an intact upcycle

The core fact of the week is a divergence: the sharpest two-day semis flush since March 2025 landed in the same
72 hours as the strongest fundamental news of the cycle — Samsung hiking Q3 DRAM contracts up to 20%, a record
prelim consensus, HBM sold out through 2027, DRAM undersupplied through ~2028. When price and fundamentals
diverge that hard, one of them is wrong, and three pieces of evidence say it's price:

1. **VIX stayed ~16 through the entire flush.** A 13% two-day sector drop with headline vol asleep is the
   signature of a single-sector de-gross, not a risk-off. Systemic events don't leave the index vol surface
   untouched.
2. **NVDA fell ~1% while MU fell >10%.** The purge concentrated precisely in proportion to prior momentum
   (MU +260% YTD, SOXX +82%) — that's profit-taking mechanics, not an information event. Bad news about AI
   demand would hit NVDA hardest, not least.
3. **SK Hynix did −14.6% then +10.9% in consecutive sessions.** Fundamentals don't oscillate 25% in 48 hours;
   leverage does. This is the same Korean leveraged-ETF machinery documented on Jun 23 (single-stock ETFs ≈14%
   of Samsung/SKH turnover), running its third flush-and-snap in ten days on a tape where two chip stocks are
   half the index.

So the framework verdict repeats the wiki's June forensics: **flows, not fundamentals** — the third such event in
three weeks (Jun-23 Korea ETF crash → Jun-30 quarter-end rebalance → Jul 1–2 rotation purge). The
tripwire dashboard confirms: forward EPS-revisions pointing up (prices
being *hiked* 20% into an undersupplied 2027), contract pricing up, hyperscaler capex guides not due until late
July. **Nothing fundamental has flipped.**

**Where the wiki's framework was wrong — grade it honestly.** The [Jun-29 positioning read](../positioning/unwind-exhausted-or-just-starting-2026-06-29.md)
put the second leg at ~40%, *gated on a hot jobs print*. The second leg fired anyway — **on a weak jobs print**.
The trigger wasn't rates; it was crowding itself: after +80% H1 gains, record leveraged-ETF AUM, and two stocks
becoming half of KOSPI, profit-taking is self-triggering. The vulnerability diagnosis was right, the trigger
condition and the probability weight were wrong. Lesson for the framework, filed here: **in a sufficiently crowded
tape, "flush exhausted" is an unstable state — no macro catalyst is required for a re-fire.** The Jun-30
duration note's other half held up well: the de-gross tail was predicted to run into mid-July, and that is
exactly the window that just produced the violence.

**On the Great Rotation.** Tech → Dow profit-taking is the persistent flow ("the blue boring names attracting
inflows directly from recent profit-taking money"), and it's why the Dow made records on the same days semis
flushed. But the weak NFP subtly undercuts the rotation's macro leg — it removed the hike threat that made
long-duration tech relatively unattractive — while the sector's earnings news got *better*. Rotations out of a
sector with accelerating earnings and 20% price hikes historically exhaust when a dated fundamental confirmation
forces money back. This week supplies three dated confirmations: Samsung prelim (Tue), TSMC June sales (~Fri),
and the setup into next week's CPI (Jul 14) / ASML (Jul 15) / TSMC Q2 (Jul 16) cluster.

**Why Tuesday is different from the Jun-24 Micron trap.** The wiki's own lesson from MU's print: a record that is
already consensus isn't news — beat-and-raise produced a marginal ATH close, then a −22% round trip, because
positioning was maximum-long into it. Samsung's prelim carries the same priced-in risk (₩85.5T *is* the
consensus)… but the positioning is now the mirror image: the complex just took its sharpest two-day flush in 16
months, momentum longs are cleaned out, and Korea enters the print one day off a −8% intraday crash. **Same-quality
news into opposite positioning flips the asymmetry.** A consensus-or-better prelim into a de-grossed tape has far
more room to lift than the MU print did; only a genuine miss (hard to square with Samsung simultaneously demanding
+20% on contracts) re-opens the downside.

## Tuesday's prelim: how much is priced in, and how much can it move? (written Mon, before the print)

The user's challenge: *"the market knows memory brought record earnings — how much is priced in?"* The honest
answer needs three different rulers, because "priced in" means three different things:

**1. In consensus terms: the record is 100% priced — by construction.** ₩85.5T *is* the FnGuide consensus; the
bar IS the record. Nobody gets paid tomorrow for Samsung merely printing an all-time high — that outcome is the
midpoint of the distribution. Sharper: the consensus revenue/OP pair implies a **50.5% operating margin**
(85.5/169.4) vs 42.7% in Q1 — the market has already written down **Samsung's first-ever 50%+ OPM quarter, a
+780bp QoQ expansion**. That is the operational bar. And after Friday's +20% Q3 contract-hike headline, the
whisper sits *above* the published consensus — call the effective bar **₩86–88T**. A print of exactly 85.5 is
"in-line" on paper and a hair light vs whisper.

**2. In valuation terms: the record's LEVEL is priced; its DURATION is priced at ~zero.** Samsung at ~₩310k is
roughly ₩2,000T ≈ $1.3T of market cap against ~₩342T of *annualized* consensus OP — a mid-single-digit multiple
of operating profit, high-single-digit P/E. The market pays a **cyclical-peak multiple for new-era numbers**:
it fully believes Q2, and does not believe the run-rate survives. (Same structure as SK Hynix at ~7.6× and MU at
~10× forward — the [cyclical-vs-new-era](../memory/cyclical-vs-new-era.md) verdict, visible in the multiple.)
This is why Friday's *Q3 pricing* news moved the stock +8.2% while the long-known record Q2 consensus moved it
nothing: **all the tradeable variance is in duration, not level.**

**3. In flow terms: the print has been partially pre-traded from both sides.** Friday's +8.2% monetized part of
the good news early (the hike headline is *forward* information, more valuable than the backward-looking Q2
number), while the Jul 1–2 flush pre-paid the sell-the-news risk (momentum longs already cleaned out). The two
roughly cancel — the print-day reaction should be *cleaner* than usual, more about the number and less about
positioning than the Jun-24 MU print was.

**Base rates (from training, approximate — the sprint's base-rate-table rep):** over the past two years, Samsung
prelim-day moves clustered **±1–3.5%**, and the *sign inverted naive beat/miss logic roughly half the time* — the
Jan-2025 big miss rallied ~+3% (bad news pre-priced), the Jul-2024 big beat closed red (sell-the-news). Prelims
are only two numbers, telegraphed by monthly Korea export data and weekly contract-price trackers, so the day
mostly trades positioning, not information. Conditional on the current regime (−9%/+8% consecutive sessions,
realized vol far above the two-year norm), scale that base rate up ~1.5–2×: **expected |move| ≈ ±3–4% in Seoul,
fat tails both sides, right-skewed** because positioning is freshly de-grossed.

**The pre-registered distribution (Seoul day-of; US read-through Tuesday):**

| Print (OP) | Prob | Seoul reaction | US semis read-through |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beat >₩90T** | ~15% | +5–8% gap; KOSPI +2%+; forces FY-revision cascade | MU/SKH-sympathy +3–5%; SOXX reclaims ~$600 |
| **₩86–90T (meets whisper)** | ~35% | +2–4%; bounce scenario A confirmed | +1–3%; MU tests $1,052 from below |
| **₩83–86T (in-line, light vs whisper)** | ~30% | −1 to +2% chop; tape decides on H2 narrative by the close | Muted; watch Seoul's close, not the headline |
| **Miss ₩78–83T** | ~15% | −2 to −5% — **but decompose first** (below) | −2–4%; $975 retest risk |
| **Big miss <₩78T** | ~5% | −6%+; the flows-thesis gets a real challenge | Scenario C activates |

**Two decomposition tools for the morning (the prelim is rev+OP only — no divisions, no call):**
- **The margin back-out:** OP/rev vs the 50.5% consensus ratio is the single best real-time tell. Margin ≥50%
  on in-line revenue = pricing running ahead even of the hike narrative (duration-bullish, buy the dip if Seoul
  opens red). Heavy revenue with sub-49% margin = volume/mix doing the work (duration-neutral).
- **The provision look-through:** one-off employee-incentive provisions are the classic Q2 noise term in Samsung
  prelims. A small headline miss that the street attributes to provisions/FX by mid-session historically gets
  bought back; a miss that backs out to *soft memory ASPs* — hard to square with Samsung simultaneously demanding
  +20% for Q3 — would be real. Watch which explanation Korean brokers converge on by the Seoul close.

**Clock mechanics for a US book:** the prelim lands ~08:30 KST Tuesday = ~19:30 ET **Monday, after the US close**.
The US decision point is Monday's close; the full Seoul session (Mon 19:30 ET → Tue 02:30 ET) is the overnight
verdict; Tuesday's US open inherits both. Seoul's *close*, not the headline, is the better predictor of the US gap.

**The cap on the whole event:** two numbers can confirm the bounce; they cannot re-rate the complex. The prelim
carries no HBM4 detail, no divisional OP, no call — everything that could move *duration* (the only thing not
priced) arrives later: full results Jul 23, SK Hynix Jul 29, hyperscaler capex Jul 28–30, and next week's
CPI/ASML/TSMC. **Tuesday is a gate, not a destination** — it decides whether scenario A starts, not whether the
re-rating happens.

## MU back to the $1,200s — the recovery-path math (asked Mon Jul 6, from $975)

The gap: $975.56 → $1,200 is **+23.0%** (+24.3% to the $1,213 record close, +28.6% to the $1,255 intraday ATH).
Two separate questions hide in "when": can vol carry it there, and what makes the drift positive.

**The vol clock says "any month."** MU's regime daily σ is ~4–5% (recent tape: −13, +15.7, −6.7, −10 in single
sessions), so monthly σ ≈ 20–23%. By the reflection principle, even at **zero drift** the odds of *touching*
+23% within a month are ~30%, and >50% within a quarter. $1,200 is a 1σ-monthly move — distance is not the
obstacle. The obstacle is drift: after a broken support, driftless vol cuts both ways ($750 is also 1σ away).

**The drift comes from one engine: FY27 EPS revisions — and the fuel already exists.** At $975 MU trades almost
exactly **10.0× forward FY27 (~$97.77)**. Two roads to $1,200s: the multiple re-rates 10→12.3× (requires the
duration evidence the market has refused to pay for all cycle — possible but the *hard* road), or **FY27 EPS
revises +23% to ~$120 at a constant 10×** (requires only that current pricing flows into models). Here is the
key arithmetic: at memory's current ~60% operating margins, a +20% ASP increase on flat bits lifts operating
profit ~+33% (elasticity ~1.6–1.7×, operating leverage on fixed costs). **Samsung's +20% Q3 contract demand, if
it sticks even partially, funds the entire +23% EPS revision on its own** — no re-rating, no change of the
market's cyclical mind required. That makes the revision road the high-probability path, and it runs on a
known schedule: sell-side model updates over the next 4–8 weeks as Q3 contracts settle, then MU's own FQ4
print (~late Sep) forces the mark-to-market.

**The timeline scenarios (pre-registered):**

| Path | Prob | $1,200s when | What has to happen |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fast** | ~30% | **late Jul – mid Aug** | This week bounces (scenario A) + Jul 14–16 CPI/ASML/TSMC confirm + SKH Jul-29 HBM4 strong → revisions arrive early |
| **Base** | ~35–40% | **around the FQ4 print, late Sep** | July chops (rotation persists), revisions grind up through August contracts + NVDA late-Aug, MU's own print is the catalyst |
| **Slow** | ~15–20% | **Q4 2026** | Great Rotation extends a full quarter; only hyperscaler Q3 guides + holiday-quarter pricing force it |
| **Derate** | ~10% | **not at this cycle's multiple** | A tripwire actually flips (contract prices roll, a hyperscaler cuts capex) — the −22% becomes the start of the derate, and $1,200 recedes as E falls |

Probability-weighted: **the modal window is August–September, with MU's late-September FQ4 print the single
highest-probability date-certain catalyst** if the fast path doesn't fire this month.

**Leading indicators to watch, in order:** (1) the direction of sell-side FY27 revisions over the next 2–3
weeks — if the +20% hike starts showing up in models, the road is open (this is also the playbook's master
tripwire); (2) a daily close back above **$1,052** — the broken support is the first gate, and $1,200 talk is
premature below it; (3) NVDA relative strength (thesis-health tell); (4) whether the SKH ADR debut pulls US
money back into memory or out of MU specifically; (5) the monthly DRAM contract prints.

**The anchoring warning (the wiki's own medicine):** $1,213 was the price *eight trading days ago*, which makes
$1,200 feel like "normal" — that's a recency anchor, not analysis. The playbook's discipline is that
re-entry/adds key off tripwires and revisions, not off recovering a remembered price. If revisions stall at
$97, $1,200 isn't "due back" — it was a 12.4× print that the tape paid for one afternoon. Price targets are
outputs of E × multiple, and only E is forecastable here.
