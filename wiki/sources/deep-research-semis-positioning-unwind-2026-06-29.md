## Verdict — LEANS EXHAUSTED / bounce-prone, but NOT an all-clear (confidence: LOW-MODERATE)
The **acute, *measurable* volatility/de-risking event is largely passing** — but the **structural leverage that
amplified it is still loaded**, so a **second forced-de-risking leg is a genuine conditional tail, gated on Thursday's
(Jul 2) jobs print.** It is *not* "just starting" in the sense of an active fresh leg already underway; the current
vol evidence says the flush is **exhausting**. Rough odds: **base case ~55-60% stabilize/bounce-prone; ~40% a second
leg** if a hot jobs print trips the still-full systematic/leveraged complex.

**The key asymmetry:** the **bounce-leaning evidence is *current* (Jun 26) and *survived* adversarial verification,
while the bear-leaning evidence is either *stale* (mid-May, ~6 weeks old) or *abstained on the quota* — and the
specific "fear-is-still-steep" claims were REFUTED.** That tilts the read toward "passing," with the bear tail resting
on a still-loaded structure we can't fully measure now.

## Evidence FOR "exhausted / bounce-prone" (current + verified)
- **The IV-crush happened (single-name event passed).** MU pre-print front-week IV was *maximal* — **155.32%,
  IV-Rank 100**, steep event backwardation (front-week 155% vs 17-Jul 108% vs Aug 102%, a ~47-pt premium) — which
  mechanically collapses post-print. *(measured; 3-0)* — note this **corrects the wiki's "~103% pre-print" figure**:
  ~103-108% was the post-event monthly tenor; the *event* front-week sat at 155%.
- **SOXL vol normalizing, skew only modest.** At Fri Jun-26 close, SOXL 30-day **realized 237.75% > implied 187.76%**
  — options price forward vol *below* what just realized (not pricing continued panic); put/call IV ratio only **1.046**
  (downside skew present but *not* panic-steep). *(measured; 2-0)*
- **VIX curve calm-ish.** Only a *mild* front-month backwardation blip Jun-23 (Jul future 18.45 vs spot 19.49); **VIX9D
  16.29 BELOW spot**, and 3M/6M/1Y slope **up** (contango). A near-term-event blip, not systemic stress. *(measured; 3-0)*
- **Idiosyncratic, not systemic.** 3-month implied correlation **COR3M 8.55** (near range lows) with dispersion
  **DSPX 43.55** (elevated) → a *sector-specific* rout, not market-wide forced de-grossing contagion. *(measured; 3-0)*
- **The bear *vol* tells were REFUTED.** "CBOE SKEW 143 / steep downside skew," "SMH put/call OI ~3:1," "SMH put/call
  volume 8.35," and the "±11% event straddle" claims all **failed verification (0-3)** — do not assert that fear/skew
  is still extreme.
- **Low short interest → long de-risking, not a short raid.** **MU SI 3.70% of float, days-to-cover 0.8** (Jun-15
  settle, +10.77% vs prior). Tiny squeeze fuel, but also confirms the drop was **longs trimming** (which can stop on
  its own), not shorts attacking. *(measured, MarketBeat)*
- **Core flows only marginally negative.** SOXX −$221M trailing-5d vs **+$2.92B 1M / +$6.98B 1Y** → a marginal
  reversal, **not** a capitulation. *(abstained on quota — directional color)*
- **Base rate leans bounce.** Momentum/panic flushes occur in *post-decline, high-vol* states and are **contemporaneous
  with rebounds** (Daniel-Moskowitz, NBER). *(2-1)* Plus a 3-week-old **analog**: the same complex V-bounced after the
  Jun-5 Broadcom-miss flush (Jun-8: Intel +11%, MU +9.9%).

## Evidence FOR "just starting / more de-risking" (the bear tail — mostly stale or conditional)
- **Record short-gamma amplifier still loaded.** US levered-ETF AUM hit **~$203B (mid-May, +49% since end-March), 92%
  equity / 70% tech** — "synthetic short gamma" that *exacerbates* downside (Citadel). *(3-0, but mid-May / stale)*
- **CTAs full and skewed to sell.** Citadel CTA model at extremes mid-May (Nasdaq highest since Oct), **flow asymmetry
  skewed down — projected sell >> buy.** *(2-1, mid-May / stale)*
- **The originating-shock complex was huge & growing INTO the shock.** Korea/HK **SK Hynix 2x to ~HK$95.9B (+41.5% in
  4wks)**; CSOP SK Hynix 2x ~**$17B**, HK's largest ETF (Jun-22). It took a **−24-26% mechanical hit Jun-23**, but the
  **post-crash unwind status is unverified**, and the **regulator is now weighing curbs** (forward forced-unwind risk).
  *(abstained + search)*
- **Complex-level IV still elevated.** SMH IV **57.19%, IV-Rank 97.5, IV-pctl 99** → the *broad-complex* vol event has
  **not** fully passed (distinct from the MU single-name crush). *(measured; 2-1)*
- **HFs were already de-grossing semis going in.** GS Prime: semis the **most net-sold US subsector** (~May 21-22).
  *(3-0, stale baseline)*
- **Quarter-end mechanical selling** still live Mon-Tue (JPM ~$165B into Jun-30; from the prior brief). **The catalyst:
  June jobs Thu Jul 2** → a hot print could trip the loaded CTA/levered complex.
