# Trade Checklist — Holding & Exiting at New Highs

> Education, **not investment advice.** Run this on any name you hold (or are about to buy) **at/near new highs.**
> It exists to stop you being **the late, weak hand a pullback shakes out** — the structural reason holding through
> new highs keeps hurting: your cost basis sits right at the shakeout level, while early buyers have a fat cushion
> and calmly sell into your buying (the [disposition effect](../../shared/concepts/disposition-effect.md)).
> **No item here calls the top.** This is **condition-recognition + position-management, not prediction** — a strong
> reading means *trim and tighten risk*, never "sell, the top is in." The four tells are documented in full on
> [distribution & pullback tells](../../shared/concepts/distribution-and-pullback-tells.md).

## The four tells, at a glance (for the "while-holding" step)
Read them **together** (any one alone is weak); **breadth is the most decisive.** Full write-up (why each
discriminates + the catch) → [distribution & pullback tells](../../shared/concepts/distribution-and-pullback-tells.md).

| Tell | ⚠️ Warning (distribution-suggestive) | ✅ Benign (keep holding) |
|---|---|---|
| **Breadth / divergence** | new high **alone**, peers rolling over | the whole complex confirms the high |
| **Volume / demand** | new highs on **fading** volume; rallies sold, dips not bought; churn | dips bought, volume building on strength |
| **Structure** | first **lower high** / failed breakout | higher lows holding above the breakout |
| **Crowding / vol** | IV-rank ~100, leveraged-ETF crowding (→ violent **if** it breaks) | calm positioning, mid-range IV |

## The checklist

### ☐ Before you enter
- [ ] **Early or late?** A quiet base, or an *already-obvious* breakout? If it's obvious, you're the late buyer by construction — demand a real reason, don't chase.
- [ ] **Written forward target?** A thesis + price ($E × multiple → $T). No target = no anchor to hold against chop.
- [ ] **Priced-in catalyst near?** (consensus already above guidance, IV-rank high) → plan the trim **now**, not in the moment.
- [ ] **Survivable size?** Can a thesis-break exit happen without forcing you out? ([risk of ruin](../../shared/concepts/risk-of-ruin.md))

### ☐ While holding at new highs — run the four tells
- [ ] **Breadth:** confirming with peers, or diverging alone?
- [ ] **Volume:** dips bought / volume building (hold) — or rallies sold / volume fading / churn (warning)?
- [ ] **Structure:** higher lows holding (hold) — or first lower high / failed breakout (warning)?
- [ ] **Crowding:** IV-rank maxed + leverage crowded? → a pullback would be violent; size for it.
- [ ] **Timeframe:** am I judging this on the **daily/weekly**, or panicking on the 1-minute?

### ☐ Trim / exit triggers — pre-commit the rule, don't improvise
- [ ] **Extended into a priced-in catalyst** → trim a defined tranche **into strength, before the event** (the rule that worked on MU: $1,255 → ~$1,070).
- [ ] **Forward target hit + froth/distribution tells firing** → trim / exit.
- [ ] **Thesis tripwire rolls** (EPS-revisions flatten, contract price m/m negative) → exit — a real **break**, not a dip.
- [ ] **Clean break of the consolidation floor / key MA** → exit (hard price invalidation).
- [ ] **NOT a trigger:** one red day · a round number · "it feels toppy" · being underwater on a late entry. That's noise — acting on it is *how you get shaken out.*

### ☐ After
- [ ] **Journal the exit reason:** objective tripwire or nerves? Was I the late weak hand? Feeds the [no-chase rule](../strategies/conviction-swing-on-institutional-accumulation.md) — the only thing that durably closes the leak.
